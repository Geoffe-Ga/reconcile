# Strange bedfellows: which band? (#428)

Four invented readers, fourteen invented shelf entries, and every pair scored on the device by the spike's kinship method. A score is the share of what an entry says (its words, and its two-word phrases at double weight) that one of the reader's own Documents also says: 0 means nothing in common, 1 means the reader already says all of it. The Bedfellows view would show entries whose score falls inside a band, from its low up to (not including) its high.

Every group, person and place is made up. "A person's reading" is how I marked each pair *before* any method ran, with one exception, noted under "passing" below.

## The three methods tried

Each method reads both texts into terms, drops stop words, numbers and words under three letters, and scores the share of the entry's terms that the reader's closest Document has. They differ in whether word endings fold ("tracking" meets "track") and whether two-word phrases count extra ("coal plant").

| Method | Lowest "shares one thing" | Highest unrelated | Lowest near copy | Can a band split them? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exact words | 0.15 (wren × No tracking apps in our schools) | 0.10 (wren × Hunters for the Okker: children, defend) | 0.90 | yes, a floor between 0.10 and 0.15 |
| Stemmed words | 0.17 (wren × No tracking apps in our schools) | 0.14 (ash × Chess in the park: boards, winter) | 0.90 | yes, a floor between 0.14 and 0.17 |
| Stemmed words + phrases (recommended) | 0.13 (wren × No tracking apps in our schools) | 0.10 (ash × Chess in the park: boards, winter) | 0.89 | yes, a floor between 0.10 and 0.13 |

Without the stop list (tried in the prototype only), every pair shares "the", "and" and "every", and unrelated pairs reach 0.29, above most of the shared-one-thing pairs.

## What the method can't see

- **one thing, other words:** wren × Don't shutter the Elm Street reading room scores 0.05 (reading).
- **same subject, other side:** pell × Let the deer winter in peace scores 0.18 (deer, rifles, state, lower, land).
- **one thing, in passing:** ash × Clean the Okker River scores 0.09 (coal plant).
- **Which sense a word has:** "boards" in the chess park meets the housing "board" in ash's platform (ash × Chess in the park, 0.10), which is why the default floor sits above 0.10.

The passing mention was first marked "shares one thing". No band on these samples takes it in without also taking "boards, winter", so it stands apart: band C shows what taking it in costs.

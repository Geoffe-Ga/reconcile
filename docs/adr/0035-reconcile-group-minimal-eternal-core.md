# ADR-0035: Reconcile-the-Group Starts from a Minimal Eternal Core

- **Status:** Accepted; the core's exact wording needs the founder's sign-off before genesis
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Decided via:** `/decide` sheet `2026-11-07-reconcile-remaining`, decision `reconcile-bootstrap`
- **Builds on:** [ADR-0013](0013-covenant-on-reach.md) (covenant), [ADR-0015](0015-anti-capture.md) (eternal Atoms), [ADR-0016](0016-suspend-then-vote.md) (stewards)

## Decision

- The founder writes only **a single eternal Atom**: no calls for violence and no dehumanization. Draft wording, to confirm before genesis:
  > Reconcile does not carry calls for violence against people, or speech that denies any group's humanity.
- **Everything else in the covenant starts as ordinary or entrenched Atoms.** The first groups ratify them by vote, and they can be amended later.
- **Assumption (stated, not asked):** a group needs a steward from day one. The founder holds the first steward seat until the first groups ratify the covenant and elect stewards. The seat converts to an election once a threshold written into Reconcile-the-group's genesis is reached (for example, 5 groups or 12 months, whichever comes first).

## Why

An eternal clause can never be fixed. The less one person writes into permanence, the smaller the capture surface at the outermost group (ADR-0001, tension 3).

## Consequences

- The eternal wording is irreversible once genesis is signed. Review it with counsel and with pilot groups before Phase 2.
- Must be settled before Phase 2 discovery (ADR-0038) or any reach enforcement ships.

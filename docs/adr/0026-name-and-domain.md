# ADR-0026: The Name "Reconcile" with a Distinctive Mark; reconcile.earth Proposed

- **Status:** Accepted (domain proposed)
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Decided via:** `/decide` sheet `2026-11-07-reconcile-product-stack`, decision `name`

## Decision

- Keep **Reconcile**, registered as a distinctive mark rather than the bare word, after a USPTO search in classes 9 and 42.
- **Proposed domain: `reconcile.earth`.** "Reconcile Earth" could itself be the distinctive mark. A word plus a domain has been registered as a trademark when people treat it as a brand (*USPTO v. Booking.com*, 2020).

## Considerations for the Domain

- **For it:** short, memorable, and it echoes holonic promotion's outermost group (everyone, the whole earth) and the earth palette in `DESIGN.md`.
- **Against it:**
  - People will type `.com` by habit.
  - Some email filters and corporate proxies distrust less familiar top-level domains.
  - Renewal prices for niche top-level domains can be high or rise later.
- **Before buying:** check the renewal price, not just the first year, and whether reconcile.org or reconcile.app is available as a redirect. Make sure the mark you file matches what you put on the domain.

Buying domains and filing trademarks spends money, so both are the user's call.

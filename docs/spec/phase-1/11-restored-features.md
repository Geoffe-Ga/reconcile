### Personal page (#93)

ADR-0074 builds the personal page at level 2: a personal mission statement on this device, and a read-only share link (#105). A personal statement may be the most identifying thing anyone writes here, so it stays local-first ([ADR-0001](../../adr/0001-local-first-trust-model.md)): it leaves the device only inside the passphrase-sealed backup file (§3), #97's encrypted relay backup, which reuses the same snapshot, and #96's sealed second-device link bundle, which takes the snapshot through `withoutShares` and so leaves out #105's share state. The one other way out is the share link (#105, below), which the person turns on for one Document at a time: then its latest version goes to a mailbox of the share's own, signed and sealed. No group's record carries it, and nothing else sends it anywhere.

- **Store** (`client/src/store/personal.ts`). Store version 4 adds a `personal` object store of `{id, title, text, createdAt, updatedAt}`, plus `share` while the Document is shared (#105), keyed by `id`: 16 random bytes in unpadded base64url. The upgrade adds the store beside the groups and deletes nothing. `listPersonal` lists them oldest first, and `savePersonal` refuses one whose id, title or times aren't well formed (`isPersonalDocument`): a title is trimmed, 1–120 code points by `nfcLength`, and `updatedAt` is never before `createdAt`.
- **Personal** (`#/me`, `client/src/screens/Personal.tsx`), reached from a card on Start, behind `FirstRunGate` like every screen. It lists the device's Documents by title, each opening at `#/me/{id}`, then New document. With none yet, it offers My mission statement with one line, "What do you stand for, and what are you working toward?". Either opens a new id, and nothing is stored until that Document's first save. One line says "They stay on this device. Nothing here is sent to the relay or to your groups, unless you share one by link or send one with a request to join. A backup file you save includes them." (#425), or, once the device keeps a relay backup key (#97, §3), "They stay on this device. Nothing here goes to your groups. A backup file you save includes them, and so does your sealed relay backup." (#248). It follows the key as it comes, and shows only once the store has said whether there is one, since a wrong line is worse than none. A shared Document's card says "Shared by link" under its title (#105), or "Link paused after a restore" while a restore has paused its share (#328), or "Link paused after 30 days without an update" once this device last posted to it 30 or more days ago (#339).
- **Editing** (`#/me/{id}`, `client/src/screens/PersonalDocument.tsx`). A title field, then the composer's Write and Preview tabs and hint (§8.7), with no why, no link and no Propose, since nobody votes on a personal Document. Its text is one field, edited directly, with no Atoms, so #75's Add a section here, Propose removing this section and Copy as Markdown don't apply: a section is a blank line in the text, and the text is already Markdown to copy. The first Document on a device starts titled My mission statement, with its prompt line; another starts with a blank title. The text saves as typed about 500 ms after typing stops, and when the editor closes, with "Saved on this device." after each save. Preview renders `normalizeAtomText(text)` through `<AtomText>`, so normalizing never moves the cursor, and #105 normalizes before it posts. A blank title or one over 120 code points is refused, tied to the field, and holds saving with "Not saved yet. Fix the title to save your changes." Each save keeps the first save's `createdAt` and stamps `updatedAt` with the device clock, never earlier than the last stamp. Delete opens an alert dialog, "Delete '<title>' from this device? A backup you saved earlier still has it.", and drops any save still waiting before it deletes, so none can bring the Document back. Under the fields a line says "This stays on this device. Nothing here is sent to the relay or to your groups, unless you send it with a request to join. A backup file you save includes it." (#425), or, once the device keeps a relay backup key, "This stays on this device. Nothing here goes to your groups. A backup file you save includes it, and so does your sealed relay backup." (#248). A shared Document's line says instead that a sealed copy of its latest version waits on the relay for its link (#105), and names the sealed relay backup the same way, unless its share is paused (#328, #339): then nothing is sent, and the unshared line stays. Like Personal's, the line waits for the store's answer.
- **Deferred past the MVP:** a public profile, following a person, listing your groups on your page, and keeping personal Documents in sync between linked devices (restore a backup instead). Linked devices' relay backup keeps every Document either holds (§3, #247), but neither device takes in the other's.

### Share link (#105)

ADR-0074's personal page "used as a resume", at level 2: a read-only link to one personal Document, which anyone can open in any browser without installing Reconcile or joining anything. It shows only that Document's latest version, with no follow, no name and no groups. Edits reach the link, and Stop sharing turns it off. The relay's 30-day retention ([ADR-0057](../../adr/0057-environments-updates-retention.md)) is the only expiry, so a share that stays on is posted again.

- **Keys** (`client/src/crypto/share.ts`, pinned by `src/crypto/vectors/share.json`, computed outside @noble). Share link makes a random 32-byte seed and a fresh Ed25519 keypair, used for this share alone. The share mailbox id is the unpadded base64url of `hkdf(sha256, seed, empty salt, "reconcile/v1/share-mailbox", 32)`, and the seal key the same with `reconcile/v1/share-key`. The link is `${origin}/#share/${seed}.${publicKey}`, two 43-character base64url values joined by a dot, so both travel only after `#`. The public key is in the link because every reader holds the seal key and could post a copy: only copies signed by the owner's key count. Each share gets a fresh seed, mailbox and keypair, so nothing the relay stores ties it to the person's groups. Sharing the same Document again makes a new link.
- **Copies** (`client/src/share/copy.ts`). A copy is UTF-8 JSON, `{v: 1, n, title, text}` with the text normalized (`normalizeAtomText`), or a tombstone `{v: 1, n, stopped: true}`. It is signed with `sign` under `reconcile/v1/share`, framed as a record (`publicKey ‖ signature ‖ bytes`), sealed with the app's `Seal`, and posted with `putBlob(mailbox, blob, 30)`. A reader parses only exactly these fields. A Document whose record, at the largest `n`, would be over 64,000 bytes can't be shared (`fitsShare`), and a message tied to Share link says so; a shared one that grows past it says its link keeps the last version that fit.
- **Posting** (`client/src/share/shareLink.ts`). Before each post the device reads the share's mailbox and numbers the copy one past the highest `n` there that verifies under the share's key, or its own last `n`, whichever is higher, so restoring an older backup can't roll the link back. A verified tombstone there is final: the device drops the share and posts nothing, so a backup made before Stop sharing can't bring the share back while the relay keeps the tombstone. Posts for one Document run one at a time, so two never take one `n`.
- **A restored share starts paused** (#328). A tombstone lasts only as long as the relay keeps it, 30 days, and a backup made before Stop sharing still holds the share as live. So every share a restore brings back, from a backup file, from the relay copy (#97, #118) or on the unlock screen (#95), is kept with `paused: true` (`restoredCopy`), a flag on the share's record that no backup carries, and nothing posts to its link: not `keepSharesAlive`, not leaving the editor after an edit, and not `postCopy`, which says `paused`. Two shares are left as they were: the device's own share, kept when the backup's later copy holds none or holds that same share while the device's is live, and any share made after the restore. #96's link bundle carries no share, so it pauses nothing. The editor's Share by link section shows no link while paused. It asks "This link was live when the backup was made. Keep sharing?", with "Nothing goes to the link until you choose. Keep sharing sends it this Document's latest version.", then Keep sharing and Stop sharing, both secondary, so neither is pressed on the person. Keep sharing (`keepSharing`) records the choice, then posts the latest copy, which still reads the mailbox first. Posted, it shows the link with "Still shared. The link shows this Document's latest version." A tombstone still there drops the share and posts nothing, with "This link had already been stopped, so it stays off." A relay it can't reach keeps the choice and leaves the share pending for the next launch, with "Still shared. The link hasn't caught up with this Document yet." Stop sharing posts only a tombstone, as for any share. Offline, the share stays paused, with "Sharing couldn't be stopped, so the link stays paused. Stopping needs the relay. Check your connection, then try again." Deleting the Document still stops the share first. On a device whose keys are locked with a passkey (#255), a paused share's mark keeps the pause, so Personal still marks it and its editor says instead "This link was live when the backup was made, and nothing goes to it until you choose. Your keys on this device are locked with your passkey. Close Reconcile, open it again and unlock it to keep sharing, stop sharing or delete this Document.", with no Keep sharing, Stop sharing or Delete. Keep sharing or Stop sharing in a tab whose keys another tab locked sends nothing and says so, as Share link does, and the share stays paused.
- **A share idle for 30 days is paused too** (#339). Two devices can hold one live share, as when a restored device kept sharing while the device that made it still holds it. If the person stops sharing on one, the other, not opened until the tombstone has lapsed, would find nothing in the mailbox and post the Document to the old link again. A device that hasn't posted to a share for the tombstone's lifetime can't tell a live link from one stopped elsewhere. So a share whose `lastPostedAt` is 30 or more days old (`SHARE_TTL_DAYS`, the copies' own lifetime, so `mayStillOpen` is false) waits for the person exactly as a restored one does (`pauseOf`): not `keepSharesAlive`, not leaving the editor after an edit, and not `postCopy`, which says `paused`. Any Stop came after this device's last post, so its tombstone outlives those 30 days, and a share posted less than 30 days ago still finds it. The rule reads the device clock that stamped `lastPostedAt`, and stores nothing new: the pause follows from that time. The section asks "This device hasn't updated this link in 30 days, so it may have been stopped on another device. Keep sharing?", with the restored share's note and buttons, and Personal's card says "Link paused after 30 days without an update". A restored share asks the restore's question, however old its last post. Keep sharing stamps `lastPostedAt` with the time of the choice, so a post that can't reach the relay goes at the next launch, unless that launch is 30 or more days later, when the device asks again. Otherwise Keep sharing and Stop sharing behave as for a restored share. While the device's keys are locked with a passkey (#255), the editor says instead "This device hasn't updated this link in 30 days, so it may have been stopped on another device, and nothing goes to it until you choose. Your keys on this device are locked with your passkey. Close Reconcile, open it again and unlock it to keep sharing, stop sharing or delete this Document.", with no Keep sharing, Stop sharing or Delete.
- **The owner's side.** The share state `{seed, secretKey, publicKey, n, lastPostedAt, pending}` (`lastPostedAt` is when this device last posted, or the person kept sharing, #339) lives on the personal record, so the backup file and #97's relay backup carry it (§3), never the `paused` flag a restore adds; #96's link bundle doesn't, because personal Documents don't sync between devices and two devices posting one share would roll its link back and forth. A linked device starts with every Document unshared, and only the device that made a share can stop it (§3, Second device). Only `updateShare` sets or drops it: the editor's saves keep it, and mark it `pending` when the title or text changed. On a device whose keys are locked with a passkey, its seed, signing key and public key live in the vault (#255, §3), so while it's locked the editor says "This Document is shared by link, but your keys on this device are locked with your passkey. Close Reconcile, open it again and unlock it to see the link, stop sharing or delete this Document.", with no link, Share link again, Stop sharing or Delete; Share link, Stop sharing or Keep sharing (#328) in a tab whose keys another tab locked sends nothing and says "Your keys on this device are locked with your passkey, so nothing changed. Close Reconcile, open it again and unlock it, then try again." A restore whose later copy of a Document holds no share keeps the device's, marked pending (`restoredCopy`), so no live link is lost where the person couldn't stop it. Off by default, the editor's Share by link section says, before anything is sent, "Share link makes a link anyone can open in any browser to read this Document's latest version, without joining anything. They see its title and text, never your name, your groups or your other Documents." and "The relay keeps only a sealed copy it can't read. As when your groups sync, it sees this device's internet address while it posts, so someone watching it live could tell this device also uses your groups." Share link saves what was typed, posts the first copy, and only once the relay has it keeps the share and hands the link to `shareOrCopy` with fixed words, "Something I wrote, shared read-only with Reconcile:", never the title or text. Offline, nothing is kept or copied. Beside the link: "Anyone with this link can read this Document. It stops working if you don't open Reconcile for 30 days." Leaving the editor posts a new copy when an edit is pending (`postEdits`), and any failed post sets `pending`. `keepSharesAlive()` runs at each launch and posts the latest copy of every share that is pending or was last posted 20 or more days ago, unless it's paused. Stop sharing posts a tombstone, then drops the share, and says "Sharing stopped. People who opened the link may have kept a copy, and earlier copies stay on the relay, still sealed, until they expire within a month." Offline, it changes nothing and says so. The relay has no delete route, and this adds none. Deleting a shared Document stops sharing first, and deletes nothing when it can't.
- **The reader's page** (`client/src/screens/SharedPage.tsx`). `#share/…` renders it outside `FirstRunGate`: a fresh page gets it alone (`SharedRoot`), with no store, sync loop or service worker, and an open app shows it in place of first run. It parses the link, GETs the mailbox (paging) and opens each blob, keeps only copies whose record key is the link's and whose signature verifies, and shows the one with the highest `n`, ties going to the later seq; any verified tombstone means the share has stopped. The title shows as plain text in `<bdi>` and the text through `<AtomText>`, under "Shared with Reconcile. Read-only.", and the tab reads "Shared page · Reconcile", never the Document's words. A tombstone, an empty mailbox, no valid copy and a damaged link all show "This page isn't shared any more, or the link is damaged.", and a damaged link makes no request; a relay it can't reach shows Try again. The page opens no IndexedDB database, makes no key, writes no web storage and starts no sync loop. It leaves the fragment in place, so a reload works, and once the address leaves the link the page reloads into the app.
- **Privacy.** The text leaves the device only padded and sealed (ADR-0019), under a key that exists only after `#` in the link. As with any sync, the relay sees the poster's and each reader's IP address live and doesn't store it. Posts run from the same IP address as group syncs, so whoever watches the relay live can tie the share to those groups (ADR-0075 §2); the disclosure says so. Anyone who read the link may keep what they read.
- **Deferred past the MVP:** following a person, showing their groups or Proposals, short links, view counts and comments.

### Rules the restored features share

These come from ADR-0074's "Rules this needs":

- **Windows** (#94, #106). Ordinary votes run 3–14 days (default 7) and entrenched votes 14–28 (default 14). The deadline is fixed in relay time when the Proposal opens, and early close still applies.
- **Bars** (ADR-0015, #94). Entrenched sections, dissolution and widening an opt-in pass at an average of at least 3.0 with under 5% zeros. Removal has its own bar (below).
- **Rules are fixed at genesis** with ADR-0067's Fellowship defaults (#94, below). The one exception is the opt-ins that [ADR-0040](../../adr/0040-group-opt-ins-are-governed.md) governs, the chat bot and the front door: a rules Proposal widens them at the entrenched bar and narrows them at the ordinary bar (#117).
- **Coalitions form by private, group-to-group invitation,** with no public index, and nest up to 3 levels (#100, #122).
- **One more network destination:** a Discord webhook, only for a group that turned its bot on by Proposal. The URL lives only in the encrypted group state (#119). The CSP's `connect-src` allows the app's own origin and `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/`, and nothing else.
- **Recovery uses a random backup key,** and contacts hold Shamir shares of it (#97, #109).
- **A webhook URL is a credential** (#117). It exists only inside sealed group events and in folded state on members' devices, so the relay never sees it readable, and screens show only its last 4 characters. Only `postWebhook` and `getWebhook` (`client/src/net/discord.ts`, #119, #123) call it, and only while the group has its bot on. What a device learns of it (`chatChannel`, #123) is keyed by the webhook's id, never its token.

### Founding rules

#94 builds them in `client/src/engine/rules.ts` ([ADR-0051](../../adr/0051-dissolution-and-rule-classes.md), [ADR-0067](../../adr/0067-genesis-templates.md), ADR-0074). `DEFAULT_RULES` holds whole numbers only, and `RULE_CLASS` gives each field its ADR-0051 class:

| Field | Default | Class |
|---|---|---|
| `bars` | ordinary `{avgAtLeast: 2, zerosUnderPercent: 10}`, entrenched `{avgAtLeast: 3, zerosUnderPercent: 5}`, removal `{avgAtLeast: 3, zerosUnderPercent: 5, zerosAllowed: 1}` (#115) | entrenched |
| `windows` (days) | ordinary `{min: 3, max: 14, default: 7}`, entrenched `{min: 14, max: 28, default: 14}`, removal `{min: 14, max: 14, default: 14}`: fixed at the entrenched default (#115) | ordinary |
| `quorum` | `{min: 3, percent: 30}` | entrenched |
| `vouches` | 2 (#101) | ordinary |
| `waitDays` | 14 (#101) | ordinary |
| `ballotExpiryDays` | 30 (#111) | ordinary |
| `stewards` | `{firstElectionAt: 3, secondSeatAt: 6, termDays: 182}` (#102) | entrenched |
| `suspensionDays` | 7 (#102) | entrenched |
| `optIns` | `{chatBot: null, frontDoor: false}`: both off (#117) | `opt-in`: widening entrenched, narrowing ordinary (ADR-0040) |

- **Genesis.** Create group writes `rules: DEFAULT_RULES` into genesis, and the fold keeps the result in `state.rules`. A missing field, at any depth, takes its default, so a genesis without `rules` (every one before #94) still founds its group. A field that differs from its default (the text `"2"` differs from the number 2) makes the genesis invalid: the MVP sets no rule but the defaults, so a hand-made genesis can't lower a bar, and a later change to a default must keep accepting the old value. A field this engine doesn't know is left out of `state.rules`, so a newer device's genesis still founds the group, and the engine that knows the field checks it.
- **Classes.** `targetClass` gives each Proposal its class when it opens (§4), and the class picks its bar (§5) and bounds its window: the proposer chooses one within `windows[class]`, and a Proposal without one runs `windows[class].default` (#106). Removal votes ignore `window` and run a fixed 14 days, `windows.removal` ([ADR-0075](../../adr/0075-safeguards-for-the-revised-mvp.md) §3, #115).
- **Opt-ins** (#117). A genesis restates them off like any other default, so no group is founded with its chat bot on or its front door open. A rules Proposal changes the standing value afterwards (§4, Opt-ins), and `state.rules.optIns` holds it.
- **Later fields.** #115 added removal's bar and window. A genesis from before it, which has neither, takes their defaults, and an older engine leaves both out as fields it doesn't know.

### ADR-0075's safeguards

- **Stewards and removal** (§3; #90, #101, #102, #106, #115):
  - A suspension never shrinks the electorate. Eligibility ignores suspensions (#90's `wouldVote`), and a suspended member can vote once the suspension ends, if the vote is still open.
  - A steward holds at most one active suspension, and a steward facing an open recall can't suspend or narrow. The 30-day guard between suspensions of one person runs from the end of the last one.
  - A removal passes when `sum ≥ 3·n` and either `zeros ≤ 1` or `20·zeros < n`, so a single 0 never blocks it. Removal votes run a fixed 14 days, so a suspension that a removal vote extends lasts at most 21 days.
  - "Voting members" means the people who hold voting rights, whatever level they chose, counted by #101's `votingRightsCount`. The bootstrap and the steward thresholds both use it.
  - **Steward seats** (#102): 1 seat under 6 voting members and 2 from 6, with staggered 6-month terms ([ADR-0049](../../adr/0049-stewards-and-removal.md)). That's the Fellowship default. A Circle keeps 1 seat until it reaches 10 voting members (ADR-0067, ADR-0072 item 8), but Circles stay deferred (§12), so every MVP group gets the Fellowship seats.
- **Opt-ins** (§3, §5; #117, #121). A steward's narrowing is valid only beside the same steward's open off-Proposal, and lasts until that Proposal closes. `isWider` compares only what outsiders can see or reach, so replacing a webhook or rotating a front door's keys is an ordinary change.
- **Push** (§2; #89, #99, #110). Push is off by default, with one switch per group. The switch's disclosure says that whoever runs the relay can see that one phone is in each group where push is on. The relay keeps each opted-in mailbox's endpoints and due minutes for at most 30 days, and nothing else about push (#89, §6). This is an accepted risk, and #130 revisits it.
- **Recovery** (§4; #78, #96, #97, #109, #118).
  - The random backup key travels in the backup file and in the second-device link bundle, with its split's random id and never the contacts, so a restored or linked device keeps the relay backup fresh. One person's latest choice of contacts wins on every device: it makes a new key, and every device holding the replaced one stops refreshing it once it finds the old id overwritten, which the choosing device keeps overwritten (§3, #272).
  - Linked devices keep one relay copy, which carries a group only one of them joined after the link, so a restore brings it back whichever device uploaded last (§3, #327). A device carries such a group sealed, as the copy held it, and checks it in memory only as a restore would, never keeping, folding into its own state or syncing it. A group the device that held it left, removed or wiped stays out.
  - Anyone else holding the backup key, a backup file and its passphrase or, after #109, colluding contacts, could plant a group entry or a personal Document that doesn't check out. A restore, from a file, the relay copy, a link or recovery with contacts (#118), leaves out each such entry or Document, brings back every other group and Document, and counts how many of each it left out, which a file's restore and a link say; the carry and the merge leave it out too, so the next refresh writes the copy without it (§3, #336, #351). Recovery with contacts shows no such line, for groups or Documents: it shows no count of groups at all, and a person who finds something missing can ask their group for it again. A wrong key, a copy that doesn't open, a format it doesn't read, or a groups or personal field that isn't a list still fails the whole restore.
  - The 72-hour wait runs on each contact's device, in relay time from the relay's receipt of that contact's alert.
  - The picker says "Pick people you'd trust with all your groups at once.", and that a contact picked in two groups can tell both names are the same person.
  - Colluding contacts can skip the wait. This is an accepted risk, and #131 revisits it.
- **Coalitions** (§1, §5; #100, #124, #126).
  - A promoted Dispatch counts as passed in its origin group only when that group's own fold holds the passed Proposal with the same id, title, text and ceiling.
  - A coalition speaks only through passed motions.
  - Following a coalition posts nothing, so followers can't see each other.
  - Anyone holding a seat secret can block a motion or act in the coalition. This is an accepted risk, and #129 revisits it.
- **Dissolved archives** (§5; #108). No relay time arrives after a dissolve, so each device clears the archive's ballots, notes and vouches (#222) 30 days after it, and with wipe erases the archive 7 days after it, by its own clock at launch (§7, Dissolved archives). This is the one place device time decides anything.

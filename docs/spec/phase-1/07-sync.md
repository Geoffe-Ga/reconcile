## 7. Sync

#64, #71 and #73 build it.

- **Mailboxes** (#110; ADR-0019, ADR-0074). Each group writes to one mailbox a week, derived from its current epoch's secret and the relay week (§3, #98), so the relay sees an unrelated id each week; each invite gets a short-lived one of its own (ADR-0073). Records read from any of an epoch's weekly mailboxes are tagged with its epoch. Group events are posted with `ttl_days=30`, so the relay keeps them 30 days (ADR-0057), and 5 weeks cover everything it still holds. Push registrations and nudges take their mailbox only from `mailboxAt(group, minute)` (`client/src/store/mailbox.ts`, #99): the current secret's id for that minute's week, so a key change moves them to the new epoch's mailboxes and each week to its own. Per-member mailboxes, batching and jitter stay deferred (ADR-0073).
- **Polling.** While the app is visible, sync runs every 3 seconds, and at once on focus or when the browser comes back online. After a failure it doubles its wait, up to 30 seconds; coming back online resets the wait (#168). There's no WebSocket.
- **Each pass:**
  1. GETs first (#110): the mailbox of the week of the group's last relay minute, or week 0's, which no one writes to, while the group has none yet (a new group's first pass). That GET's `now` gives this week, so a device back from a long absence never writes to a stale week;
  2. posts the outbox in order to this week's mailbox. An entry the relay took (a 2xx) leaves the outbox only in the store change that saves the pull reading it back (step 4; #343, #349), so an event made offline survives a reload and every screen shows it pending, then folded, never neither; a pass that fails between the PUT and that pull posts it again, and dedupe by event id folds it once. A `proposal`, or a `vote` after which this device's fold, with the coalitions' motions its seats decide, closes its Proposal early (#284), goes with `wake=1` (`wakeFor`, `client/src/engine/wake.ts`, #99), and so does a `dispatch` (#112), whether or not this device's own push is on;
  3. reads each week `pollWeeks` names, ascending: last week's mailbox and this week's, so a record that reached last week's as the week turned is still read, and, when the last sync fell in an earlier week, each week since, once, back to the last 5. Each mailbox is read after its own cursor (the first GET stands for its week, read on past what it held when this device has posted there), paging until a page holds fewer than `limit` records. The pass opens each blob, skips one that won't open, and adds each record that verifies and whose event id isn't in the log yet, the weeks' records merged by seq. A record read in a later pass than records of higher seq, as one that reached last week's mailbox after this device read it, goes before them: within one relay, a higher seq never arrived earlier (`client/src/store/logOrder.ts`);
  4. saves each mailbox's cursor, by its id, for the last 5 weeks, dropping older ones; `relayNow`, the earliest of its GETs' `now`s, so no record a GET left out arrived before it; `instance`; and the time of the last successful sync, dropping from the outbox, in the same change, each entry the relay took in step 2. The group's base `cursor` is where a mailbox with no cursor of its own is read from: 0 from creation, the invite's highest seq after a join, and the key change's seq after a move to a new epoch;
  5. reads the new epoch's mailboxes at once when the pull moved the group to a new epoch (§4, Encryption keys and a new group key; #98), queues an `unwrap-failed` when the pull brought a key change that doesn't check out here (#224), unless the group still reads only, as a restored copy does until it hears from its group (#231), and queues the member's `kx` when the fold has none;
  6. runs the chat bot for the group (§4, Chat bot; #119), which claims and posts only while the group has its bot on and hasn't dissolved, after `settleDissolve` and `settleRemoval` (Dissolve and Removal, below), and never once the loop is halted (#78). Nothing it does changes whether the pass succeeded;
  7. keeps the group's front door (§4, Front door; #121): its page when one is due and its inbox while the door is open in effect, and a closed or final page for each door no longer open (#288), a dissolved group's included, never once the loop is halted. Nothing it does changes whether the pass succeeded;
  8. keeps the group's listing on the public shelf (#486, [`listed-doors.md`](../listed-doors.md) §4.5; `client/src/shelf/keeper.ts`), never once the loop is halted. For each door the group ever listed by a passed Proposal: while that door is open in effect and listed, the open listing, `{v: 1, kind: "listing", open: true, name, skeleton, covenant}`, with the group's name (cut to 119 code points and "…" past 120), the name's skeleton (`nameSkeleton`, #487; [`06-relay-api.md`](06-relay-api.md)), and the covenant the door is listed under; otherwise, once the door closed in effect for any reason (a vote, unlisting, a rotation, a steward's narrowing, a membership's end (#288) or a dissolve (#108)), the final listing, `{v: 1, kind: "listing", open: false, final: true}`. Before posting, the device reads the whole shelf and takes the copy that stands at the door's address as every device folds it: a final copy outranks every other, else the newest. It posts when none stands, when the standing copy's text differs, or when that copy is 10,080 relay minutes old; it never posts an open listing where a final one stands, nor after the relay answers `409 listing_final`, so relisting takes a new opening Proposal and a new door. It remembers in memory what it found current, so it reads the shelf once a launch, and again only when a listing changes or comes due; while the relay has no shelf it posts nothing and asks again after 60 relay minutes. Nothing it does changes whether the pass succeeded;
  9. posts the push nudges for this device's own open Proposals, and for the coalitions' open motions its member may vote on (#284; §7, Push), each once, and none for a read-only archive (#108). Nothing it does changes whether the pass succeeded either.

  After a pass in which every group synced, each group with a key change due gets the duty (§4). While one is due, the outbox sends only `key-rotate`, `leave`, `unwrap-failed` and `key-taken`. A read-only archive (Dissolve, below) gets neither: no key change is due once a group has dissolved, and an archive writes none and posts no `kx`. Then, unless the loop is halted, push upkeep runs (Push, below), then the replies to the front-door applications this device sent (§4, Front door; #121), which runs on a device in no group too, and last the relay backup's refresh (`afterPass`, §3 Relay backup, #97), so both see each group at the epoch its key change moved it to; the refresh may send the day's largest request, so it waits for the rest.
- **A new `instance`**, at a pass's first GET, means a fresh relay database: the pass reads every mailbox from 0 and posts the outbox to it, its pull starts every cursor over and puts its records after the whole log, since the fresh relay's seqs start again from 1, and dedupe by event id keeps any event from folding twice. A pass that meets another instance after its first GET fails, and the next starts over.
- **Leave** (#77). Leave this group signs a `leave` and `markLeaving` queues it after the outbox and marks the group `leaving`, in one store change. From then on the store queues nothing more for the group, and the screens read "Leaving…", with proposing, voting, level changes and Copy invite link turned off. Once a pass's PUT of the leave returns a seq, the pass reads the group's mailboxes once more, to see where the leave landed (#225, §4 Stranded leaves), then `purgeGroup` deletes every record of the group from the device in one transaction (its log, cursor, outbox, keys, secret, drafts and flags all live in its one record), then `dropCoalitionsOf` purges each coalition entry only that group held (#278, §4 Coalitions), and the pass reads nothing more for any of them. When that read brings a key change that came before the leave and checks out here, the device first moves and posts the leave again to the new epoch's mailbox. A pass that fails after the PUT leaves the group leaving, its leave still queued, as every entry stays until its pull (#349): the next pass posts it again, which folds once, and reads and decides the same way. Offline, or while the relay refuses it, the leave waits in the outbox across reloads. Clearing the group from the person's other device when it syncs a leave made elsewhere is deferred: they tap Leave there too.
- **Dissolve** (#108, `client/src/store/archive.ts`). After a pass that brought new records or a new relay minute, `settleDissolve` folds the group. Once a `dissolve` has folded, `markArchived(groupId, 'dissolved')`, and then `dropCoalitionsOf`, as for a removal (#278), drops the outbox, any drafts, any leave, the chat bot's claims and what it learned of the webhook (#123), and marks the group archived, in one store change; from then on the store queues and claims nothing for it, and a pass sends and reads nothing for it and its chat bot posts nothing, so no request names its mailbox again and the relay's copy expires on its 30-day schedule (ADR-0057). There's no relay route to delete it: a `DELETE` keyed by the mailbox id would let any member, or anyone holding an unexpired invite, erase events other devices haven't fetched. Before that, a device whose member's `dissolve` the fold would take, folding a dissolve Proposal's final pass, queues one, unless one is queued already or the group is leaving. Erase's halt (#78) waits for a running pass, whose group still settles, since that touches only the store, and runs no chat bot after it. #115 reuses `markArchived` for removal.
- **Removal** (#115, `client/src/store/removal.ts`). After `settleDissolve`, the same pass runs `settleRemoval`. Once a `remove` naming this device's own member has folded, it runs push's `forget` for the group, as Leave does, which notes the `DELETE`s of its registration that upkeep sends within the next day (#99, #356; §7 Push), then `markArchived(groupId, 'removed')`: the outbox, drafts, any leave, chat claims and what it learned of the webhook (#123) go, and from then on a pass sends and reads nothing for the group, so no request names its mailbox again but those `DELETE`s, each once. Each coalition entry only that group held on the device is purged (`dropCoalitionsOf`, #278), with each coalition above that only those reached (#122) and each one's room on the next pass (`keepRooms`), and none is made from a removed copy, so the device keeps no coalition's secret, log or names through it; an entry another of the person's groups still holds, or reaches through a coalition it holds, keeps syncing, and so does one the person follows by its link (#124). By then the key is changing past this device (§4), so it couldn't read anything new anyway; it keeps what it already has (ADR-0016). Otherwise, when a removal's pass is final and waits for its `remove`, a device whose member `mayRemove` allows (not the member it names, not suspended), that isn't leaving and has none queued, queues one `remove`. Its next pass posts it, and the duty then writes the key change (§4). A backup file or a link bundle whose log holds this member's `remove` comes back archived as `removed`; a backup leaves out a removed group, as it leaves out a dissolved one.
- **Restored copies read only until they hear from the group** (#231, [ADR-0051's 2026-11-11 amendment](../../adr/0051-dissolution-and-rule-classes.md)). A pass reads a copy a restore brought back (§3, Backup file; #96's link and #97's relay backup alike) as any group, and sends nothing for it: no event, `kx`, chat post, front-door page, nudge, result or key change, and no push upkeep. Its expired ballots are still cleared on the device (#111). The store marks it live, in the pull's own change (`applyPull`), when the pull's relay minute is at most 28 days (`HISTORY_GAP_MS`) after `since`, so the relay still held everything since and the pass read it, or when the pull adds a record received at or after `since`; and #73's merge marks it live when the person opens a new invite link from someone in the group, since a device that keeps a group read-only for any reason makes no invite. A pass that reads a `dissolve` archives it as dissolved instead, and one whose fold holds this member's `remove` archives it as removed, with no push `forget`, since it sends nothing for the group (`markArchived` replaces the mark). The store queues and claims nothing for it meanwhile, and a backup, link or relay backup leaves it out.
- **Dissolved archives keep time on the device** (ADR-0075 §5). No relay time arrives after a dissolve, so at each launch, before syncing, `sweepArchives` acts on each archive by the device's own clock: with `wipe`, the first launch at least `WIPE_DAYS` (7) × 1,440 minutes after the dissolve's `received` runs `purgeGroup`, so everyone sees the result first; without, the first launch at least `ballotExpiryDays` (30) × 1,440 minutes after it clears the archive's ballots, notes and vouches in one change: the store keeps the state the device last folded with no ballot on any Proposal and no vouch (#222), and each decided Proposal's totals and outcome from the device's own tally (`clearArchive`), and drops the log. The screens show that kept state from then on. This is a device-local stand-in for #111's expiry, which needs a `result` in the log, and should converge on it. A removed member's copy (#115) clears the same way, at the first launch at least `ballotExpiryDays` × 1,440 minutes after the `remove`'s `received` (`removedClearMinute`): it no longer syncs, so no relay time reaches it. Clearing drops removal responses with the ballots. Remove from this device runs `purgeGroup` at any time.
- **Folded state uses relay records only.** An event in the outbox shows as pending and never in folded state (#66). While it waits, its screen reads "Sending…" if the last sync reached the relay, and "Saved on this device. It will send when you're back online." once a sync has failed; then "Sent" once it leaves the outbox, in the change that adds the relay's copy to the log (#349).
- **Joining with full history** (#73, `src/invite/envelope.ts`). An invite envelope is a header blob followed by chunk blobs, each sealed under the invite key with the group's `Seal` and posted to the invite mailbox with `ttl_days=1`, the header first. The relay's TTL is the only expiry, so the link lives 24–48 hours.
  - The header is JSON `{groupSecret, epoch, preview: {groupName, inviterName, documentTitle, excerpt}, token, chunkCount}`, with the group's current secret in base64url and its epoch (#98). A header without `epoch`, from before #98, reads as damaged. The title is the group's first Document's, and the excerpt its first Atom as plain text (#92), its Markdown taken out, cut to 280 code points plus "…". The group name, inviter name and title are cut to 120 code points plus "…" likewise. A reader refuses any field past its limit, so the header stays a few kilobytes at most, however long the group has run.
  - Chunks carry the relay-accepted log in seq order, never the outbox, as big-endian `seq (8 bytes) ‖ received (8) ‖ epoch (4) ‖ kept (1) ‖ length (4) ‖ record` entries (the epoch since #98, `kept` since #269), packed up to the `Seal`'s 65,492 bytes. `received` travels with each record, because deadlines depend on it. The 64,000-byte record cap means every record a device makes fits.
  - **Cleared Proposals** (#111, #269). `kept` is 1 on the one `result` each Proposal in the inviter's `cleared` keeps, and 0 on every other entry. The joiner's `cleared` is the Proposals those results name, in log order. A device clears a Proposal only by a result it keeps (Results and expiry, below), so the joiner's `cleared` names every cleared Proposal whose records the invite carries, a coalition motion's included, and the joiner folds exactly as the inviter does. An id no shipped record names changes no fold, so it stays behind. A mark costs one byte per entry, where #111's header listed every cleared Proposal's id, about 46 bytes each, until a long-lived group's header outgrew its blob (about 1,400 Proposals) and newcomers could no longer join. A mark is needed because the log alone can't tell a cleared Proposal from one nobody voted on, which keeps its result and no ballot too. A `kept` byte other than 0 or 1, or a mark on anything but a signed `result`, reads as damaged, and so does a header with `cleared`, from before #269. A card (#114) packs a coalition's log the same way, and marks nothing, since a coalition's log takes no result.
  - **A coalition's history** (#279, [ADR-0057's 2026-11-13 amendment](../../adr/0057-environments-updates-retention.md)). The relay keeps a coalition's mailboxes 30 days, and an entry read for the first time reads only its last two weeks, so the invite carries the coalitions too.
    - After the group's chunks come those of each coalition the group reaches (`carriedCoalitions` in `src/store/coalitions.ts`: `coalitionKeys`, nested ones through the coalition below, nearest first). Each is the coalition's relay-accepted log, packed the same way, none marked `kept`.
    - The header's `coalitions` lists them in order, `[{secret, chunkCount}]`: 1 to 32 distinct coalitions, each `chunkCount` at least 1. A coalition with no log yet isn't carried, and a group in no coalition writes no `coalitions`.
    - A coalition this device follows only by its link (#124) isn't one the group reaches, so it stays out.
    - Opening is all or nothing as before: a count that doesn't add up, a list that's empty, too long or repeats a coalition, or a coalition entry marked `kept` reads as damaged.
    - Join keeps each carried coalition the group reaches, judged with the carried logs (`keepCarried`). A new entry takes its log as a pull at the invite GET's `now`, through sync's verify and dedupe, as Bring keeps a card's. An entry the device already keeps, or that another tab makes first, merges in what it lacks.
  - Copy invite link copies the link only after every PUT succeeds. Otherwise it says so and copies nothing.
  - Opening is all or nothing. The app reads every blob in the invite mailbox: an empty mailbox (an expired link), a missing or extra blob, a blob that won't open, a header or chunk that doesn't parse, or entries with no genesis all show the expired-or-damaged line. A seed that doesn't parse shows it with no request at all.
  - A secret past epoch 0 must be the one its epoch's key change in the entries commits to, or the link reads as damaged (#98). A newcomer's device also takes it only when every wrap of that key change is the one the secret makes (`keyFits`, #372), as a member's device checks before it takes a key up or merges an invite's (#224), so an invite to a key change the group's devices refuse reads as damaged too, and the newcomer isn't stuck where the group recovers.
  - Nothing is stored before Join. Join makes a signing keypair and an encryption keypair, takes the group id from the entries' genesis, keeps the `cleared` its marks give as the group's own (#111, #269), imports the entries through the same verify and dedupe path as sync, sets the cursor to their highest seq, keeps the invite GET's `now` and `instance`, and queues the `join` with the token. The preview and the Document read before joining (#91) fold the entries with that `cleared` too, so a newcomer sees each cleared Proposal's outcome as its result records it.
  - A device already in the group (the same genesis id) merges in the entries it lacks, each in seq order among the ones it has, and refolds, with no new key and no join. It keeps the coalitions the invite carries as Join does (#279), unless it keeps the group as a removed or dissolved archive, which no coalition stays through (#278). The merge drops anything that expired with a Proposal in the device's own `cleared`, and keeps its own `cleared` as it is: a Proposal whose ballots it still holds clears on its own, 30 days after its decision. When the envelope's epoch is later than the device's and its secret matches that epoch's commit, the device takes it up as a switch does (§4, #98), so a device that missed a new key gets back in.
- **Results and expiry** (#111, `client/src/store/expiry.ts`). After a complete sync pass and its key changes (#98), once per relay minute for each group that isn't a read-only archive (folding a log checks every signature):
  - **The duty.** For each closed Proposal whose ballots it holds, with no valid result in the log once `relayNow` is past the close, the device re-syncs once, and if there's still none, its member signs one (`resultFor`) and syncs again, which posts it to this week's mailbox (#110) and reads it back. Any member's device may, and the fold keeps the first valid one. A device leaving the group (#77) posts none, since nothing is queued after a leave, and neither does a suspended member's (#102) nor that of the person a passed removal names (#115), since the fold ignores their result.
  - **Clearing.** `clearExpiredBallots` acts once `relayNow` ≥ the result's `closedAt` + `ballotExpiryDays` × 1,440 minutes (43,200: the 30 days are fixed, ADR-0074), counted from the decision, the early close or the deadline, whatever window the proposer chose (#106). In one store change it deletes the Proposal's `vote` records, every other `result` for it and any event type in `EXPIRES_WITH_BALLOTS` (`client/src/engine/expiry.ts`: #115's `removal-response`, never a `remove`) from the log and the outbox, and adds the id to the group's `cleared`. Nothing goes to the relay. It runs after every sync pass. A dissolved group's archive (#108), or a removed member's (#115), clears by its own sweep instead, since it posts no result.
  - **Nothing comes back.** Every ingest (sync's `applyPull`, whichever weekly mailbox a record comes from, #110; #73's merge; and the restore of #78's backup file, #96's link or #97's relay backup) drops such a record for a Proposal in `cleared`, so neither the relay, an invite nor an old backup can return one. An invite marks each cleared Proposal's kept result (#73, #269, above), and the backup file and the link carry `cleared`, so a newcomer or a restored device knows which Proposals it can't check. A restore keeps the Proposals either copy has cleared.
- **Catching up** (#73). When a sync succeeds more than 28 days after the previous one (by the device clock), the device sets a persisted `missingHistory` flag, and Group home says "Some history may be missing on this device. Open a new invite link from your group to catch up." Only a merge clears it, because the last sync's age resets the moment sync resumes. The merge catches up the group's coalitions too, since the invite carries their logs (#279): a device that was away while one formed reads it then.
- **Requests.** `src/net/relay.ts` calls `/v1` on the same origin with `credentials: 'omit'`, `referrerPolicy: 'no-referrer'` and `cache: 'no-store'`. The service worker never caches `/v1` (#76).
- **Offline** (#76). The app installs from `manifest.webmanifest` (`id`, `start_url` and `scope` all `/`, `display: standalone`), with icons at 192, 512 and 512 maskable, plus a 180 `apple-touch-icon`. A service worker, `/sw.js`, keeps the build on the device:
  - The build writes it from `src/net/sw.ts` (`client/plugins/serviceWorker.ts`), as one classic script holding the list of every file the build writes (fonts and `public/` included, index.html as `/`) and a version that hashes them all. It's served from the app's own origin, so the CSP needs no change. A small hand-written worker stands in for `vite-plugin-pwa`, whose `workbox-build` fails `npm audit` with no patched release.
  - Install fetches every listed file into a cache named for the version, revalidating each with the server. Activate deletes older versions' caches. Fetch answers every page load with the app shell and each listed file from that cache, and leaves everything else to the network. Nothing under `/v1` is cached or answered from a cache, and nothing is cached at run time.
  - It registers in production only, after the page loads, from a bundled module: nothing is inline.
- **Install first** ([ADR-0029](../../adr/0029-install-first-indexeddb.md), #76). On an iPhone or iPad, nothing creates keys until the app runs from the Home Screen. An Apple touch device has `iPhone`, `iPad` or `iPod` in its user agent, or `Macintosh` with more than one touch point (iPadOS); it's installed when `(display-mode: standalone)` matches or `navigator.standalone` is true (`src/platform/installGate.ts`). Once the pilot notice is accepted, and at each launch after, the app calls `navigator.storage.persist()` (`src/platform/persist.ts`: granted, denied or unavailable, never a throw), and Start says so when the browser may still clear its data.
- **Linking a device** (#96, §3). Every PUT of the handshake and the bundle uses `ttl_days=1`. While a link screen is open, it looks at its mailbox at once and then every 2 seconds, and keeps trying while the relay is out of reach. Nothing in a link touches a group's mailbox: the new device's first sync, once the groups are in, is a group's first request from it.
- **Shares by link** (#105, §11). `keepSharesAlive` runs once at each launch, beside the sync loop and never in its pass, so it has no place in the pass's order: a share has its own seed, mailbox and keys, tied to no group, so no key change, freeze while one is due, dissolve or push upkeep touches it, and it touches none of them. A share posted while a key change is due posts as usual; the relay backup's refresh still waits for the change to land, and its copy then holds the share as last posted. It passes over a paused share, which waits for the person: one a restore brought back (#328), or one this device last posted to 30 or more days ago (#339).
- **Push** (#99, [ADR-0012](../../adr/0012-content-free-push.md); the relay's side is §6). Opt-in, one switch per group, all off to start (§8.12), with no content anywhere:
  - **The notification.** The service worker answers every push the same way, never reading its data: it closes any open notification tagged `reconcile` (Safari ignores tag replacement), then calls `showNotification("Something new in Reconcile", { tag: 'reconcile' })` with no other option, and never sets the app badge (`client/src/sw/notify.ts`). A tap closes it and focuses a Reconcile window, or opens `/`, and the app syncs as it comes to the front. The app closes these notifications whenever it becomes visible.
  - **Turning a group on** runs inside the tap: `Notification.requestPermission()` before anything is awaited, then `GET /v1/push/key` (never a build constant) and `pushManager.subscribe({ userVisibleOnly: true, applicationServerKey })`, then a `PUT /v1/push/{mailbox}` for each mailbox `mailboxAt` gives from now to 30 days on and, before them, for the week of each Proposal still open, a coalition's motion included (#284), where its nudges wait (#110). Only once the relay has taken it does the store keep the group's `push` registration (endpoint, mailboxes, `instance`, relay minute), which is the switch. A denial stores and sends nothing.
  - **Turning one off** clears the registration at once and sends a best-effort `DELETE`; once no group is on, the device calls `unsubscribe()`.
  - **Leave and a removal** (#356, [ADR-0075's 2026-11-12 amendment on push unregistering](../../adr/0075-safeguards-for-the-revised-mvp.md)). Just before `purgeGroup`, or before `markArchived` for a removal, push's `forget` owes a `DELETE` under each mailbox the registration names, all due at one device time: 1 to 1,440 minutes on, drawn uniformly from 6 CSPRNG bytes (`drawBelow`, `client/src/crypto/random.ts`). The device store keeps them in one device record (`push-forgets`), the endpoint and each mailbox id with their due time and nothing else, so they outlive the purge and a reload; no backup file or link carries them. Upkeep sends those due at its first run at or after their time, never in the pass that let the group go, so the relay can't line the endpoint's unregistering up with the key change. A `DELETE` the relay took or answered with a 4xx is owed no more; any other failure keeps it owed, and upkeep rests for an hour. Once no other group has push on, the device calls `unsubscribe()` at once, as before.
  - **What wakes.** A Proposal, a ballot that closes one early, and a Dispatch (#112) go with `wake=1` (§7, Each pass). A Dispatch a Proposal carries needs no wake of its own: it rides the result's. For each of its own open Proposals, the proposer's device posts two nudges once each, once the Proposal is in folded state: `deadline − 1440` (closing soon) and `deadline` (its result, when no ballot closes it early), each to the mailbox of the week the Proposal was received in, `mailboxAt(group, received)` (`nudgesFor`, `client/src/engine/nudges.ts`). Never the deadline's own week (#110): its minute less the vote window is the Proposal's receipt, so a nudge filed there would tie two weeks' ids together at rest. For a coalition's motion open in the group, which has no proposer there, every device whose member may vote on it posts the two, each shifted by the group's nudge offset for the motion, and the relay keeps one per mailbox and minute (#284, §4 Coalitions, Push). It keeps them in the group's `nudged` list. A new motion opens from the coalition's log with no record in the group's, so the device looks again whenever the group's log or a coalition's grows. A 4xx answer is final; anything else is tried again on a later pass. Cancelling the deadline nudge after an early close is deferred: one extra wake carries nothing.
  - **Upkeep**, after each pass in which every group synced: each group with push on is registered again 7 days after the relay last took it, or when the subscription's endpoint, the relay's `instance` or the group's mailbox set changes, the old endpoint `DELETE`d first. With weekly mailboxes (#110) the set changes as this week leaves its front and a new week enters its 30 days, about twice a week, so each change re-`PUT`s every week the registration names and `DELETE`s a week it no longer names: last week's, once no open Proposal or coalition motion was received in it. One endpoint under each of them already links a group's weekly ids at the relay (ADR-0075 §2), so the re-`PUT`s link nothing more. When the endpoint stays and the mailbox set changes, each mailbox the group no longer has is `DELETE`d once the new ones hold the endpoint. When `/v1/push/key` differs from the subscription's `applicationServerKey` (a rebuilt relay), the device unsubscribes and subscribes again. When the subscription is gone and notifications are no longer allowed, every group turns off here. After a failure, upkeep rests for an hour.
  - **Across a key change** (#98). `mailboxAt` derives from the group's current secret, so once a switch moves the group to a new epoch, the next upkeep `PUT`s the endpoint under the new epoch's weekly mailboxes and `DELETE`s it from the old ones. A phone whose app stays closed stays under the old mailbox, and sleeps through what the group posts, until its app next syncs. A `key-rotate` asks no wake, so nobody who left is woken by it, and what wakes is unchanged across epochs.
  - **A dissolved group** (#108) sends no push request at all: no wake, no nudge (`nudgesFor` gives none once a group has dissolved) and no upkeep. Its registration and any nudge already scheduled are left to expire at the relay, within 30 days, since a `DELETE` would name the mailbox after the dissolve; `markArchived` forgets the registration on the device, Settings shows no switch for a read-only archive, and turning one on fails before anything is sent. Leave's `DELETE`s are still owed, and go within the day, while a key change holds the outbox, since a `leave` passes the freeze.
  - **Personal Documents** (#93) get no switch: they aren't groups, and nothing about them reaches the relay.
  - **What goes out.** Push request bodies hold only `{"endpoint"}` or `{"not_before"}`. The endpoint goes only to the relay, under the mailboxes of groups the person turned on. A backup file never holds a registration: an endpoint belongs to one device.
- **Updates** install in the background and apply on the next launch, with no prompt (ADR-0057 as cut by ADR-0073, #76). The worker never calls `skipWaiting()` or `clients.claim()`, so a new version takes over once every Reconcile window has closed.

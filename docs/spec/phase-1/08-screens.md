## 8. Screens

Built with React Aria Components and styled only from [DESIGN.md](../../../DESIGN.md) tokens ([ADR-0022](../../adr/0022-react-vite-react-aria.md)). Every string comes from the messages module. Screens meet WCAG 2.2 AA, and axe runs on each one in unit tests and in the Chromium and WebKit e2e runs (ADR-0055, #65, #67). Routes are hash routes, with no router library (#65): `#/` Start, `#/create`, `#join/{seed}` (Join, which replaces the entry with `#/join` once it reads the seed; the tracer's `#/join/{payload}` reads as Join too), `#/g/{groupId}` Group home, `#/g/{groupId}/doc/{documentId}` one Document's view, with `#/g/{groupId}/doc` the group's first, so older links still open (#92), `#/g/{groupId}/doc/new` Propose a new Document (#92), `#/g/{groupId}/doc/{documentId}/log` a Document's change log (#104), `#/g/{groupId}/propose/{atomId}` the composer, `#/g/{groupId}/add`, `#/g/{groupId}/add-first/{documentId}` and `#/g/{groupId}/add/{atomId}` Add a section (first in the group's first Document, first in that Document, #92, or after that Atom, in its Document), `#/g/{groupId}/remove/{atomId}` Propose removing this section (#75), `#/g/{groupId}/priority` Ask what to focus on (#107), `#/g/{groupId}/remove-member/{member}` the removal composer, which proposes removing a person (#115), on a path of its own since an added section's id is a Proposal's, `#/g/{groupId}/p/{proposalId}` a Proposal and its ballot (#66), `#/g/{groupId}/again/{proposalId}` the composer proposing a lapsed Proposal again (#74), which opens the composer its target needs: a change, a section's removal, Add a section (#75), Propose a new Document (#92) or Ask what to focus on (#107), `#/g/{groupId}/steward/{seat}` and `#/g/{groupId}/recall/{seat}` the steward composer (#102), `#/g/{groupId}/dissolve` the dissolve composer (#108), `#/g/{groupId}/dispatches` the Dispatches and `#/g/{groupId}/dispatch/new` Send a Dispatch (#112), and `#/g/{groupId}/rules` Group rules, with `#/g/{groupId}/rules/{optIn}` and `#/g/{groupId}/narrow/{optIn}` its composer (#117), and `#/g/{groupId}/list/frontDoor` the composer listing the front door or unlisting it (#485), and `#/me` Personal, with `#/me/{id}` one personal Document (#93, §11), `#/discover` Discover (#423), `#/steward` the steward's console, only on a device that holds the steward's key (#488; any other device goes to Start), and `#share/{seed}.{publicKey}`, a shared personal Document, read-only and outside first run, which stays in the address bar (#105, §11). Sending a Proposal replaces the composer's entry with the Proposal's. A group is stored and routed under its group id, so every device uses the same one. Tab titles never carry a group's words, or a person's own, because browser history keeps them.

1. **First run** (#76). `FirstRunGate` wraps the whole app, so every later screen sits behind it.
   - **The landing page** ([ADR-0078](../../adr/0078-landing-page-and-painted-edges.md)). A plain visit in a browser tab, on a device that hasn't accepted the notice, opens on the landing page (`src/screens/Landing.tsx`): what Reconcile is, told through case studies over a painted background. Start a Group moves on to the install guide or the pilot notice below. It stores nothing, in `localStorage` or anywhere else, so each new visit starts on it again. An invite link, the installed app and a device that has accepted the notice skip it. Once past first run, the Reconcile mark at the link bar's start opens it again at `#/about`, outside the app's frame, with "← Back to your groups" added and Start a Group opening Create group (DESIGN.md "The landing page").
   - **The install guide.** Any iOS browser outside the installed app, in-app browsers included, shows one guide and nothing else: "If you opened this inside another app, open it in Safari first.", the Add to Home Screen steps, then "Open Reconcile from your Home Screen and paste your invite link into the bar at the top." (or just the first half, with no link; a card's says "paste your card link", a follow link's "paste your follow link", a front door's "paste the front door link", and a vote link's "Open Reconcile from your Home Screen, then paste this link into the bar at the top.", #472). A plain visit offers the app's address to copy, for leaving an in-app browser. An invite link shows Join's preview card (no name field), read from the invite's envelope by the same `lookupInvite`, the link as selectable text and Copy invite link; an expired or damaged one shows Join's expired-or-damaged line instead. A reconcile card's link and a follow link (#280) show the link the same way, under "A reconcile card" or "A coalition to follow", with Copy card link or Copy follow link and no preview, since Bring a card and Follow show what the link holds before anything happens; a damaged one shows its own page's expired-or-damaged line. Every link whose seed is a secret, an invite's, a card's or a follow link's (`src/app/heldLinks.ts`), is held this way: the guide holds it in memory and calls `history.replaceState(null, '', '/')`, so the seed leaves the address bar, and it reads a newer link opened in the same window the same way. The installed app on iOS keeps storage of its own and opens at its start, so copying the link is the only way it gets there. A vote link (#472, [ADR-0079](../../adr/0079-vote-links.md)) shows its link to copy under "A vote", with Copy vote link, and stays in the address bar, since its token holds no secret; elsewhere a vote link goes through first run as a plain visit, and its screen then says whether the vote is on the device. Nothing there stores anything or makes keys. A linking or recovery code never travels in a route, so the guide never sees or holds one.
   - **The pilot notice.** Everywhere else, the notice comes before any key exists, until this device has accepted it. It covers what's stored, what the relay sees (a device's IP address while it connects, without logging it; when messages arrive; their size class; which mailboxes a device checks; and, for a group that lists its front door, that the device is in that group, since it posts the listing (#486)), that data can be lost, and that this is a test (ADR-0056). I understand records the acceptance on this device only (`localStorage`, `src/platform/notice.ts`), then the app opens. An invite link opened under the notice leaves the address bar at once (the route becomes `#/join`) and reaches Join in memory; a card's or a follow link does the same, its route becoming `#/card` or `#/follow`, and reaches Bring a card or Follow in memory (#280).
   - **The unlock screen** (#95). A device whose keys are locked with a passkey opens on "Unlock Reconcile", whose one button asks for the passkey. Nothing syncs, signs, sweeps dissolved archives or shows a group until it unlocks. When it doesn't: "Your passkey didn't unlock Reconcile.", with Try again, Restore a backup (a backup file's groups in place of the locked ones, their keys unlocked) and Erase. It never seals the keys again under another passkey by itself.
   - **The passkey offer** (#95). Once, where a passkey can lock the keys (WebAuthn, and `prfTrusted`), right after the notice, or at the next launch on a device that accepted the notice before: what a passkey protects against, that the phone asks twice the first time, and what the password manager sees, then Make a passkey and Not now. Its answer is a device flag beside the notice's (`src/platform/passkeyOffer.ts`), which Erase clears. An invite, card or follow link opened while either screen shows is held as under the notice.

   - **History** (#104; `client/src/engine/history.ts`, `SectionHistory.tsx`). Every section offers History, to every level, a leaver and a dissolved group's readers included: a React Aria Modal styled as DESIGN.md's side sheet, which focus enters and returns from. `sectionHistory(state, atomId)` replays the final passes in the fold's order from `state.founding` (the genesis's receipt, its founder, or null in a coalition, and every founded Atom's text), so it reads only the log the device holds, with no new event. `passed`, newest first, holds each pass that changed the section, `{proposalId, title, why, decidedAt, text, author, totals, groups, change}`: `text` is what the section read right after (`""` once removed), `author` the winning version's, `decidedAt` the close in relay time, and `totals` the winning version's `{scorers, sum, zeros, quorum}`, from `proposalTally`, so a cleared Proposal's come from its result (#111), or from a cleared archive's kept tallies (#108).

### Removal

#115 builds it in `client/src/engine/removal.ts`, `fold.ts` and `client/src/store/removal.ts` ([ADR-0016](../../../adr/0016-suspend-then-vote.md), [ADR-0049](../../../adr/0049-stewards-and-removal.md), [ADR-0059](../../../adr/0059-suspension-during-removal.md), [ADR-0072](../../../adr/0072-protocol-details-for-decomposition.md) §4, [ADR-0075](../../../adr/0075-safeguards-for-the-revised-mvp.md) §3). The fold and the screens ask the same functions there (`mayProposeRemoval`, `mayRespond`, `mayRemove`, `removalDue`, `removedFrom`), so a screen never offers what the fold would ignore.

The first valid one wins | #115 |

- **The vote.** A removal is its own class, `removal`: it runs `windows.removal.default`, 14 days, whatever the proposer wrote, and passes at `bars.removal`, `sum ≥ 3·n` and either `zeros ≤ 1` or `20·zeros < n` (§5), so a single 0 never blocks it (ADR-0075 §3). Its one version has no text and takes no `version` events, and the member it names is left out of its eligible set (`eligibleNow(state, member)`), so their own score never counts. One removal of a member is open at a time. Competing removal versions stay deferred.
- **The response.** `state.responses` keeps each response in relay order as `{id, seq, received, by, proposal, text}`. The fold counts code points of the text as received and never normalizes it again. The ballot shows it under "<name>'s response" (§8). It expires with the ballots: `removal-response` is in `EXPIRES_WITH_BALLOTS` (#111), and a removed or dissolved archive's sweep drops it too. A `remove` never expires, so a cleared removal keeps the person out by its result. A cleared removal is open until the early close its result gives, as #111's `isOpenAt` reads it, so a second removal of the same person in that time stays ignored. The title and justification stay in the signed log, because expiring them stays deferred (ADR-0074).
- **The pass.** Before each record, and once more at the end, the fold brings `state.removals` up to `at`, before the seats: each removal that has closed with a pass, once, in order of close time and then relay order, as `{proposal, member, passedAt, removed: null}`. From then on `can()` grants that member only `read` (`REMOVED_CAPABILITIES`), whatever their level, seat or suspension, `wouldVote` leaves them out of any Proposal that opens, and the fold ignores their events apart from their `leave` (§4, The fold).
- **The remove.** Any member's device that folds the final pass, while its member is still in the group and no `remove` has folded, posts one (`settleRemoval`, §7). The first valid one sets the removal's `removed` to `{id, seq, received, by}` and takes the member out as a leave would (`departs` in `fold.ts`): they move to `former`, any seat they hold empties, and `endsMembership` makes a key change due (§4, Encryption keys and a new group key), so the key rotates once, wrapped to everyone but them. It also closes an open front door, as a leave does (Front door, below; #288). Its duty, check and refusal are a leave's: a key change past them whose wraps don't open is refused and a fresh one follows (#224, `src/store/poisonedKeyChange.test.ts`). Ballots they cast before it still count, and open Proposals keep their eligible sets. A member who leaves first needs no `remove`, and their leave changes the key instead.
- **A suspension during a removal** (ADR-0059). A removal of a member whose suspension is running when it opens extends that suspension: `until` becomes the later of its own end and the vote's close, the deadline while it's open and the early close once there is one, and its `removal` names the vote. So a removal that fails or lapses after the suspension's own 7 days ends it then, and one opened on its last day keeps it at most 21 days in all. A vote that closes before the 7 days are up never shortens it. A removal opened before the suspension doesn't extend it, even when it closes early while the suspension runs.
- **After a dissolve** (#108) no removal opens, responds or removes, and one still open never closes.
- **The removed device** (§7). Once a `remove` naming its own member has folded, the device archives the group as `removed`, forgets its push registration (#99), and syncs it no more. It keeps what it already has (ADR-0016), and Group home says so (§8). The key change after the `remove` means nothing sealed later opens under any key it holds.
- Golden vectors: `vectors/removal-*.json` and `vectors/bars-removal-edges.json`. Deferred past the MVP: readmission ([ADR-0066](../../../adr/0066-readmission.md)), parting ways, competing removal versions, and penalties for people who vouched for the removed person.

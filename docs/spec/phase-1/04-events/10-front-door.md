### Front door

#121 builds it: the keys and boxes in `client/src/crypto/door.ts`, the invite record in `client/src/engine/frontDoor.ts`, and the page, Send, the inbox and the keeper in `client/src/door/` ([ADR-0038](../../../adr/0038-optional-discovery.md), [ADR-0014](../../../adr/0014-invite-and-vouch-admission.md), ADR-0040, ADR-0075 §5). Devices act only on `effectiveOptIn(state, 'frontDoor')`, so a steward's narrowing closes the door at once.

| Type | Body | Valid when | Built by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `door-invited` | `{application}`: the SHA-256, in unpadded base64url, of the sealed application blob an invite answered | the author holds `invite` (`can()`); the door is open in effect; `application` is 32 bytes in canonical base64url; and no `door-invited` for it has folded. The first valid one per application wins (`mayRecordInvite`) | #121 |

- **Opening.** The opt-in composer makes the door's two secrets on the proposer's device: `sign`, an Ed25519 secret key whose public half is the door's address, and `box`, an X25519 secret key. They travel only inside the rules Proposal, in the sealed log. The link is `${origin}/#door/${address}`, the 43-character address, which is no secret, so it stays in the address bar.
- **Derivations.** HKDF-SHA-256 (§3) of the address derives the page mailbox (`"reconcile/v1/door-page"`), the page seal key (`"reconcile/v1/door-page-key"`) and the inbox (`"reconcile/v1/door-inbox"`); of a reply seed, the reply mailbox (`"reconcile/v1/door-reply"`). `vectors/door.json` pins them, a signed page and two boxes, computed outside @noble.
- **The page** is UTF-8 JSON, `{v: 1, open: true, name, excerpt, count?, box}`, `{v: 1, open: false}`, or `{v: 1, open: false, final: true}` for a door closed for good (#288): the group's name (120 code points and "…" at most), the first section of its first Document as plain text cut to 280 code points and "…" (none once that Document has no sections, #92), the member count only while `showCount` holds, and the X25519 public key of `box`. It's signed with `sign` under the tag `reconcile/v1/door-page`, sealed under the page key, and PUT with `ttl_days=30`. Of the copies whose signature verifies and whose page parses exactly, readers take the newest final one, if any is, wherever it sits (`latestCopy`), and otherwise the last in relay order; no valid copy reads as closed. So once a door's final copy is on the relay, no open copy posted after it reopens that link, whether replayed from before (#277) or forged with `sign` by someone who has left.
- **The keeper** (`createDoorKeeper`, run by the sync loop after each group syncs and, for replies, after each complete pass, §7). While the door is open in effect, a member's device posts the page when the newest copy says otherwise or is 7 days (10,080 minutes) old in relay time. The standing door, while a steward's narrowing holds it shut, gets `{v: 1, open: false}` once, while its newest copy still says open, since the group may yet reopen it (`mayReopen`). Every other door the group had stand (rotated away, closed by a vote or by a membership's end, or in a dissolved group), and a door a membership's end exposed before its Proposal passed, never opens with its keys again, so it gets a final copy, posted again whenever the newest final copy is 7 days old (#288). A member who holds `invite` reads the inbox after its cursor while the door is open in effect, and no device reads it otherwise; what it reads stays on the group's record on that device (`door`), never in a backup, and goes with Leave or an archive. The device of the person a passed removal names keeps no door at all, neither page nor inbox: from the pass it only reads (#115), and once the `remove` folds, its archive drops what it read.
- **When a membership ends** (#288; the founder's decision of 2026-11-12, ADR-0038's amendment). Whoever leaves, or is removed, keeps every front door secret the log carried while they could read it, and a door's secrets don't change with the group key (#98). So the fold closes the door at the record that ends the membership, on every device, with no event of its own:
  - **Where it closes.** At the `leave`, or at the `remove`, not at a removal's pass: from the pass the person only reads, and their device keeps no door (#115). An open door's standing value becomes `false` there, and `state.doorClosure` keeps that record's `{id, seq, received}` until a door opens again. A narrowing in force then changes nothing: the door stays closed whichever way its off-Proposal goes. A coalition has no front door.
  - **Exposed secrets.** Every `sign` and `box` any record carried by then is exposed, whether the fold applied the record or not (the device's resubmitted composer included), and so is one in any record read while the key change is due, or read from an earlier epoch's mailbox after the key changed (a late writer's, #98): whoever left holds the key each was sealed under. A rules target whose `sign` or `box` is exposed is ignored, so neither the copy a late writer's device posts again to the new mailbox nor a door brought back opens anything.
  - **A vote already running.** A front door Proposal still open when a membership ends is listed in `state.exposedDoors`. It runs to its close, but its pass sets nothing, so the door stays closed.
  - **Reopening** takes a new opening Proposal, made once the key has changed. A refused key change (#224) changes no key: one stays due, so a door proposed meanwhile is exposed too, and stays so after the fresh key change (`vectors/epoch-recovers.json`). It is entrenched like any opening, and the composer's fresh secrets give the door a new address and a new link. The old link's page stays final.
  - **Applications** a device already read stay on it as they were. With the door closed, no device reads its inbox, and Group home lists none, as for any closed door.
  - **What a reader can't be shown.** A reader holds only the door's address, so a final copy protects the old link only while one is on the relay: not before the first member's device that folds the departure posts it, nor 30 days after the last one posted, as after a dissolve, nor while someone keeps the page mailbox full to the relay's cap. #366 decides whether the relay should keep a tombstone for good.
  - Golden vectors: `vectors/door-close.json`.
- **Applying** (`#door/{address}`, below in §8). Send makes a reply X25519 key and a 32-byte reply seed and keeps both on the device, in a store of their own (`sent`), sealed in the passkey vault on a device that has one (#265, §3), then PUTs `{name, message, reply: {seed, key}}` (a name of 1 to 120 code points, a message of up to 2,000, and the reply seed and public key) sealed to the page's `box` to the inbox with `ttl_days=30`. A padded box is `box.ts`'s box of a body framed as a seal's, sized so the box is one of the four blob sizes, with the mailbox id as AAD.
- **Documents with it** (#425, [ADR-0077](../../../adr/0077-discover.md) §3). When the applicant checks some of their personal Documents, the application carries `documents: [{title, text}]` after `reply`: 1 to 3, each only a Document's title (1 to 120 code points, not blank) and text, in NFC, never its id, times or share, so nothing ties one application to another ([ADR-0009](../../../adr/0009-per-group-identities.md)). Without any, the field is absent and the bytes are what they were before #425 (`vectors/door.json`'s application). The list, as compact UTF-8 JSON, may take at most 52,588 bytes (`DOCUMENTS_MAX_BYTES`): the largest padded box carries 65,460 bytes of plaintext (65,536 less the box's 72 and the frame's 4), and the longest application without documents, every code point of its name and message escaped to six bytes, takes 12,859, and `,"documents":` 13 more. So any application the form lets through fits one blob, and a short one stays in the smallest. The form refuses more, and so does the parser, which refuses the whole application on any malformed copy. The applicant's device keeps no copy. A device on a build before #425 refuses any application with a key beyond `name`, `message` and `reply`, so it drops one that carries documents rather than showing its name and message.
- **The copy on a member's device** sits on the application beside the name and message, on the group's record: never in a backup, and, under a passkey vault, kept as the rest of the application, which a locked store reads none of until it unlocks (the vault holds keys alone). Dismiss deletes it from that device; 'Send an invite' deletes it from the sender's device as the invite goes, and every other device deletes it when it folds the `door-invited`, its door open or not: the keeper does it first on each run, before any inbox request, so a read that fails can't keep it (`forgetAnswered`), and a device that reads the application only afterwards never keeps it. Search never reads it (#445). The name and message stay, so the application isn't read in again. An application nobody answers or dismisses keeps its copy, like its name and message, until Leave or an archive.
- **Answering.** 'Send an invite' makes an ordinary invite (§3), seals its link to the applicant's reply key, padded, PUTs it to the reply mailbox with `ttl_days=2`, then queues `door-invited`. For 30 days by its own clock, the applicant's device reads each pending application's reply mailbox after every complete sync pass and keeps the invite seed from the latest reply that opens; Join forgets the application once it's used.
- **Expiry** (#111). Nothing of the door's expires with ballots: `door-invited` names an application, not a Proposal, so it isn't in `EXPIRES_WITH_BALLOTS`, and applications never enter the log. A rules Proposal that opened a door opens through the same helper as every other, so its ballots expire 30 days after its close; its record, and the door's secrets in its target, stay, and its result keeps its outcome, so the door stays open.
- **What the relay sees:** padded blobs in three mailboxes it can't link to the group or to each other, and the IP addresses of the devices that post and read them, as for any sync. The page and the inbox keep one id each for the door's life, like an invite's mailbox, and don't rotate weekly as group traffic does (#110): the link is published, so anyone holding it derives every id it would rotate to, and rotation would hide nothing from them. What a fixed id lets the relay link is the door's own traffic over time: how often its page is posted and read, how many applications arrive and when, and the addresses that post or read them. Rotating the door's keys, an ordinary change, moves both to new ids, and the old page closes. A door closed for good keeps a final copy in its page mailbox, posted again every 7 days while any member's device keeps the group (#288), so the relay can see that the group behind a closed door is still about.

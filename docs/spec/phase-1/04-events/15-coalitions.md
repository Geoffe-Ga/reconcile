### Coalitions

#100 builds the engine in `client/src/engine/coalition.ts`, `crossing.ts` and `fold.ts` ([ADR-0036](../../../adr/0036-coalition-each-group-passes.md), ADR-0009, ADR-0073, ADR-0074, [ADR-0075](../../../adr/0075-safeguards-for-the-revised-mvp.md) §1), and #114 the card, sync and admission; #120 builds the coalition's own home. A coalition is a log of its own, with the same signed records, ids, relay order and relay time as a group's, whose members are groups. `fold` folds it too: a coalition's state is a `GroupState` whose `coalition` is `{status, level, declinedAt, seats, founding, formsFinal, bindings, results, motions, admitted, wraps, commit}` (null for a group), `commit` being its genesis's commitment to the room's `inner`, or null (#371); each motion's snapshot also says the seat it would `admit`, with its `kx`, or null. A group's `coalitions` lists its reconcile Proposals as `{proposal, key, name, title, atoms, outcome}`, `outcome` being `open` until its vote is final, then `pass` or `fail` (a lapse is a fail).

- **What a coalition takes.** Only `genesis`, `join`, `proposal`, `group-result`, `room-wrap` and `leave`. Every other type is ignored there, `vote`, `version`, `chat-posted`, `chat-thread` and `dispatch` included, so a motion has one version and no ballots, `kx` and key changes don't apply, and no participant sends a Dispatch of their own (ADR-0075 §1): a `leave` ends only that participant. A `join` without a seat and its proof is ignored, so only participants join, and a follower posts nothing (#124). A group's own log ignores `group-result` and `room-wrap`.
- **Coalition Dispatches** (#124; ADR-0075 §1). A coalition speaks only through passed motions: a motion's Dispatch goes out once its pass is final, at its horizon (deadline + 1,440 minutes per level, #387), since until then a contest could still turn it into a fail. The fold appends it as a group's carried Dispatch is appended (§4, Dispatches), `by: {proposal: id}`, `doc` the coalition's Document, and `received` that minute, in order of that minute and then relay order, ahead of every record received from it on. A fail, a contest or a lapse sends nothing, and nor does a motion still open when the coalition is declined, since relay time stops. In each member group the motion opens with no Dispatch (`motionsFor`), so a group's pass sends its own members none. An admit motion carries none (`vectors/follow-dispatch.json`).
- **Forming** (ADR-0036). A coalition is `forming` until every founding seat has reported `pass` on the genesis id, then `formed`, however many seats there are. A `fail` from any founding seat, a contest included, makes it `declined` at once, even after it formed: forming needs every founding group. Results on the genesis count until `formsFinal` (#250), the genesis's receipt plus `windows.ordinary.max` days (the latest a founding group's reconcile vote can close) plus 1,440 minutes per level of the coalition, and a coalition still forming then is declined there for every founding group: the close-out, which also closes the path where a holder of a seat secret forms it with a forged late pass. A founding seat posts a `fail` only once its group's reconcile vote has closed without a pass, so a provisional count never withdraws a coalition. Once declined, the fold ignores every later event and relay time stops, as after a dissolve. A `proposal` before it forms is ignored.
- **Results that cross** (ADR-0036, ADR-0075 §1). A seat counts once per motion: `fail` if it ever reported `fail`, else `pass` if it reported `pass` (`seatResult`). So a seat that reports both is **contested** and counts as a fail, in either order: anyone holding the seat secret, a former member included, can block a motion but never pass one. Results on a motion count while their receipt is before its horizon (#250, #387; ADR-0036's 2026-11-14 amendment): its deadline plus `HORIZON_STEP_MINUTES` (1,440, a day) for each level of the coalition, `horizonOf` with the level the coalition had when the motion opened there, kept on its snapshot (`motions[].level`), so an admission that raises the coalition's level never moves an open motion's horizon. A group's, at level 0, is its deadline.
- **Deciding a motion** (`coalitionOutcome`, §5). A fail is final at once, and the motion closes there (`closedAt`). A pass is final only once results stop counting, at its horizon, since until then a contest could still turn it into a fail; until then it reads as a pass that isn't final, and the coalition's Atoms don't change. A final pass changes the Atoms as an edit's does in a group. A motion not yet decided reads as a lapse, as an unscored Proposal does.
- **Motions open in every group** (`crossing.ts`). `motionsFor(coalition, seatKey)` gives `{id, coalition, seq, received, class, window, deadline, bar}` for each motion whose snapshot holds the seat, and `fold(records, relayNow, outside)` opens each in the group as a Proposal right after the group's records with a lower relay seq: relay time moves on to its receipt, its eligible set is everyone `wouldVote` lets in there, and the group's ballots, early close, deadline and `tally()` at the motion's bar apply as usual. It has `target: {kind: "motion", coalition, bar}`, one version under the motion's id, and no title, text or author: the screens read those from the coalition. It isn't the group's own, so its `number` is 0 and the group's own Proposals are numbered without it (#123), the chat bot never claims it, so it never gets a thread, and it carries no Dispatch (#112). A group that has dissolved, or isn't founded yet, opens none. `seatIn(group, coalition)` names the seat a group holds there.
- **What a group owes** (`resultsToReport(group, coalition, seatKey)`). The `group-result` bodies to post from a participant bound to the seat: on the genesis, its reconcile outcome once final, or `fail` if the genesis's name, title or Atoms differ from the target it passed; then each motion its seat decides, once its own vote is final, a lapse as `fail`. Only while results still count, only what the coalition hasn't recorded yet, and nothing once it is declined. Each body is the motion, the seat and the result, and nothing else.
- **What a group sees** (`resultMismatches(group, coalition, seatKey)`). The recorded results for its seat that differ from its own decided result, and both sides of a contested seat, each with the participant who posted it, so the group can talk it out.
- **Leaving takes the coalition with it** (#278; consent and reversibility). When the device lets go of the group that held a coalition, once the relay has its leave (§7, Leave), or archives it, removed (#115) or dissolved (#108), it purges each coalition entry only that group held (`dropCoalitionsOf` in `src/store/coalitions.ts`): its secret, keys, log, the participants' coalition names, its outbox and its sync state, in one store change each, and each coalition above that only those reached (#122); its room goes on the next pass (`keepRooms`). An entry another live group on the device still holds, or reaches through a coalition it holds whose `inner` may yet arrive in a wrap (#313), stays, and so does one followed by its link (#124), which no group holds. `src/app/coalitionPrivacy.test.ts` reads the device's IndexedDB raw after a leave and finds no trace of the coalition, with the same read finding it before.
- **A group's ballots on a coalition's motion expire as its own do** (#278, #111). A motion opens in each group beneath as one of the group's Proposals, voted on there, and only the group's pass or fail crosses. So a group's device settles its results and expiry with the coalitions' motions its seats decide folded in (`settleExpiry` in `src/store/sync.ts`): once its vote on a motion is final, a member's device posts the group's `result` in the group's own log, and 30 days after the close every device clears the ballots and notes, keeping the outcome from the result (`vectors/coalition-expiry.json`). By then results on the motion stopped counting in the coalition long before (three days after the deadline at most, at level 3), so clearing changes nothing that crosses. The coalition's own log holds no ballot, only one pass or fail per seat, so it has nothing to clear.
- **Privacy.** A coalition log holds group names, motions, its Documents, participants' coalition names, seat public keys and one pass or fail per group, never a ballot, score, count, note, secret or group key (`properties.test.ts` builds every result a group with canaries owes, and finds none of them). The coalition, inner and seat secrets live only in member groups' sealed logs, and `inner` only in seated groups' (a wrap in the coalition's log opens only with an admitted seat's secret), and the coalition checks only the seat, never a person's level in their group, which would link identities.
- Golden vectors: `vectors/coalition-*.json` and `vectors/follow-*.json` (#124), each with a coalition log and member groups' logs (`{name, logs: {coalition, A}, relayNow, expect, report?, mismatches?, unchangedWithout?}`), and the fast-check properties that a motion's outcome doesn't depend on the order distinct seats' results arrive in, and that a coalition's Dispatch goes out exactly when every seat's pass is final (`coalitionDispatch.test.ts`).
- **Push** (#99, #284; the founder's decision of 2026-11-12). A ballot that closes a motion early in a group wakes it, as for any Proposal (`wakeFor`): the send loop folds the group with the motions its seats decide. An outside motion has no proposer in the group, so every device whose member is in its eligible set posts the group's two nudges for it, once each (`nudgesFor` with the group's offset, `client/src/store/push.ts`): `deadline − 1440 + o` (closing soon) and `deadline + o` (the group's result, when no ballot closes it early), to `mailboxAt(group, received)`, the mailbox of the week the motion opened in. `o` is the group's nudge offset for the motion, a whole number of minutes from 0 to 59 that every device holding the group's current secret derives alike (`nudgeOffset`, §3 Group secret). Both nudges take the same `o`, so neither comes before its unshifted minute, nor more than 59 minutes after it. The relay keeps one nudge per mailbox and minute, so the repeats cost requests, not wakes. Every seated group's copy of a motion has the same deadline, so unshifted nudges would line the groups' mailboxes up on the same minutes in the nudges the relay stores; shifted, two groups' nudges for a motion meet only when their offsets do, about 1 time in 60. A registration names the week of each open motion, as of each open Proposal (§7 Push). A reconcile Proposal and a coalition's motion take a third nudge, the reminder (#250; the founder's decision of 2026-11-19), at `deadline − 4,320 + o` (`REMINDER_MINUTES`, 3 days), when it opened before then, posted by the devices that post the vote's other two, with the same `o`, whether or not anyone has scored it, so its minute says nothing of who has voted. Like every nudge it carries only its minute (ADR-0012); the words "If you don't vote by <date>, you won't be counted." show only in the app, above the ballot of an eligible member who hasn't scored the vote, from that minute until it closes (`reminds` in `engine/nudges.ts`). Other Proposals take no reminder. The coalition's final pass at its horizon, and a coalition Dispatch it sends, take no nudge: no group's vote settles either.
  - **Accepted: the same hour.** Each group's nudges for a motion still fall within the hour after its two minutes, so at rest the relay sees every member group's mailbox take a nudge within the same hour on two days 1,440 minutes apart. At the pilot's scale, with few nudges an hour, that is a weaker signal than the same minute, and the founder's decision accepts it.
  - **Accepted: live timing.** Each member group's devices read a new motion within a pass or two of each other, and post its nudges then, with no random delay, so whoever watches the relay live can see several groups' mailboxes take nudges at about the same moment. A delay on nudges alone would hide nothing: a member's device already polls its groups' and its coalitions' mailboxes in one pass, from one IP address, so the shared coalition mailbox ties each member group's devices to the others live. That is the live timing ADR-0075 §2 accepts ("polling links a phone's groups live") and the pilot notice discloses (§9: whoever runs the relay sees connecting IP addresses and timing live). The offsets protect what the relay stores, not a live watcher.
- **The card** (#114, `src/coalition/card.ts`, `src/app/reconcile.ts`). Group home's Coalitions offers "Reconcile with another group" to whoever holds `propose`. It asks for the coalition's name (1–120 code points), its first statement (split at blank lines into Atoms, titled "Mission statement"), why, and "Your name in the coalition" ("This name is only shown in this coalition."). Submit makes the coalition secret, `inner` and the group's seat, queues the group's reconcile Proposal ("Reconcile into <name>"), keeps the coalition's entry with the maker's name and a plan to join under the group's seat, then PUTs an M2-03 envelope to the card mailbox, `ttl_days=1`: a header blob `{secret, inner?, seat: {name, key}, name, title, atoms, chunkCount}`, `seat` being the group's name, cut to 120 code points and a "…" as an invite's preview is, and its public seat key, never its secret, then the chunks of a log, none for a coalition still to form. Only a card that founds a coalition carries `inner`; a card from a formed coalition's row carries none (#313). Only once the PUT succeeds does it show the link, read-only with Copy, beside "Anyone who opens this link in the next 2 days can read the coalition from then on. Send it only to someone in the group you want to reconcile with." If the PUT fails, the Proposal stays queued and Try again posts the same card.
- **A card at a front door** (#426, §4 Front door). A group with an open front door also receives cards there: "Propose reconciling with this group" on its page makes a card as above, for a group the person picks, and leaves its seed in the door's inbox. It arrives in that group's Applications under "Groups asking to reconcile", where a member who may propose brings it to a vote by Bring a card's own path, or dismisses it for the whole group. Hand-to-hand card links stay.
- **Bringing a card.** The link, or the link bar (#444, `linkKind`), opens Bring a card, which takes the seed out of the address bar, reads the card mailbox, and reads the coalition's mailboxes for last week and this one (`readRecentLog`), which hold any genesis written since the card was made. It shows the card as plain text: "From <group>", the statement's title and sections, never Markdown. A card that doesn't parse, or would found a coalition but carries no `inner`, shows "This card has expired or is damaged. Ask the group that sent it for a new one." and fetches nothing more; a coalition that is forming or was declined says "This card has already been used.". Otherwise the person picks a group on this device where they hold `propose` that holds no reconcile Proposal with this secret, in its log or outbox, and types their coalition name. Bring queues that group's reconcile Proposal with the card's name and statement, under a fresh seat. For a coalition still to form it also writes the genesis, by the bringer's coalition key, with the card's statement as its one Document (#92), naming the card's seat then this group's, with `DEFAULT_RULES`, and joins under this group's seat; the first genesis wins. A card made from a formed coalition's row carries the coalition's log, so the device that brings it reads the coalition whatever the relay has dropped, and admits the group instead: the group's reconcile Proposal carries no `inner`, even from a card that does (#313); once it passes, the bringer's device proposes the admit motion with its seat's proof and `kx`, and plans to join under the seat once it's admitted.
- **Sync and results** (`src/store/coalitions.ts`). Each coalition secret a group on the device holds, in its log or outbox, gets one store entry (`kind: "coalition"`), under its entry id, with a fresh signing key for that coalition alone (ADR-0009) and the person's name there, none until they choose one. The sync loop reads and posts it as a group's (§7), weekly mailboxes and cursors included, after every group has synced, with no `kx`, chat bot, nudges, key changes or dissolve, and asks the relay to wake no one. A declined coalition is read no more. After each pass, for each coalition whose genesis has arrived: a planned join goes once the coalition names the planned group's seat; a participant bound to a seat queues what its group owes (`resultsToReport`, folding the group with `motionsFor`), leaving out what already waits; a planned admission goes once its group passes, or is dropped once it doesn't; and a participant bound to a seat whose group here holds `inner`, the one the genesis commits to, wraps it to each admitted seat they haven't wrapped for themselves, in the log or the outbox (#313, #371). Whatever that queues is posted at once. Each of these acts for a group only while this device's member still does there (#377, `actsFor`): from the final pass of a removal of them (#115), they only read, so their device reports no result or promotion record for its seat, joins under it, proposes it or wraps for it, though the `remove` that archives the group here may fold later; their own device never posts it. A suspension doesn't stop it, since a steward's word mustn't silence the group's seat, and neither does a step down to Member. The maker joins when the genesis appears; anyone else in a member group who holds `propose` joins from the coalition's row with Take part and a name. A group's own fold, everywhere the screens show it, takes the motions its seats decide, and a motion takes its title and why from its coalition. Coalition entries go in the backup file and a second device's link bundle beside the groups, under their entry ids, once their genesis is back; their plans don't. A group's invite carries the log of each coalition the group reaches (#279; §7, Joining with full history), so someone who joins a seated group after the relay has let the coalition's genesis go still reads it and can take part, and a member's device that was away while it formed catches up by opening a new invite.
- **What each group sees.** A reconcile Proposal shows the coalition's name and statement, then "Reconcile with <other groups>" once the genesis names them. Group home's Coalitions lists each coalition the group proposed or asked into: "Forming" with "Waiting for <group>'s vote" for each seat that hasn't passed and "<group> didn't pass this" for each that failed, or "Waiting for another group to bring the card." before a genesis; "Formed" with its member groups; or "Didn't form" with who didn't pass, or, at the close-out, "Not every group voted in time, so the coalition won't form.". The group's own waiting line adds " · Currently not passing" while its reconcile vote is open, at least one member has scored it, and its count isn't a pass (#250): read from the group's own ballots on this device, so it never shows for another group, whose count only it holds. Once another founding group's fail has declined the coalition, the group's reconcile Proposal, still open, shows "<group> didn't pass this, so the coalition won't form." in place of its ballot (`withdrawnBy`), and no reminder; no device can close that vote without a new event in the group's log, so on screen it is withdrawn. A participant reads "You take part as <name>" and may make a card for another group. Wherever `resultMismatches` isn't empty for the group's seat, `<ResultMismatch>` says "The result reported for your group doesn't match your group's vote."
- **The coalition's own screens** (#120, `src/app/coalitionHome.ts`, `src/engine/coalitionProgress.ts`). A formed coalition's row on group home opens its home, `#/c/{coalitionId}` (its genesis id): its open Proposals, each with its progress line and deadline; its Documents, each at `#/c/{coalitionId}/doc/{documentId}` (today its one, whose id is its genesis id, as a group's first Document's is, #92); its Decided list, each final motion with "Passed" or "Didn't pass" and the day the coalition decided it (a fail's receipt, or a pass's horizon); and its member groups, "Your group" beside each one a group on this device holds. Each motion opens as its vote in a member group on this device whose seat decides it. Someone bound to a member group's seat who holds `propose` in that group proposes from the Document (`#/c/{coalitionId}/propose/{atomId}`): the group composer's form, with the voting time and no Dispatch, which queues the motion in the coalition's outbox under the entry's key. Someone else in a member group of a formed coalition who holds `propose` there is offered Take part first, and anyone else only reads. A motion only rewords a section, so a coalition's Document offers no add, removal or Add your version, and a section with a motion open says so. Each section's History and the Document's change log (`#/c/{coalitionId}/doc/{documentId}/log`) read the coalition's own log as a group's do (#104, §8 Document view), except that a passed motion lists each group with "Passed" or "Didn't pass" in place of totals, is dated when the coalition decided it, the receipt of the result that failed it or its horizon, and opens its result in a member group on this device whose seat decides it, or nowhere; its founding text names no one. In each member group a motion is tagged "Coalition vote · <coalition>" in its lists and on its page, and its one version is a word diff against the coalition's section, named by its proposer's coalition name. `coalitionProgress(coalition, motionId, mySeat?)` reads only the coalition's state: `{seats, passed, failed, needed, deciding, mine?, outcome?, groups, final, settles}`, `outcome` being `coalitionOutcome`'s once it isn't open, and `final` true at a fail or once a pass is past `settles`. Its line, up to 4 groups or for a motion that needs every group: "2 of 3 groups have passed. Waiting for <groups still deciding>."; from 5: "Needs 4 of 5 · 3 passed, 1 didn't"; once the viewer's group has reported: "Your group passed this. Waiting for 1 more group."; and once the coalition decides, its outcome sentence. The coalition's result then leads the motion's page in each group: a final pass reads "The coalition passed this. It now reads:", the new text, and thanks to its proposer by coalition name; a pass not yet final says the day it takes effect (#250); a fail reads "Not every group passed.", or from 5 groups "Fewer than two-thirds of the groups passed."; then each group with "Passed" or "Didn't pass". Each group's own result, with its ballots and notes, stays below it on that group's members' devices alone, and a group's lapse on a motion says the coalition counts it as not passing. No count of people, ballot, note or voter's name from any group appears on a coalition's screens.
- **Following** (#124, `src/coalition/follow.ts`, `src/app/follow.ts`; ADR-0003, ADR-0021, ADR-0053, ADR-0075 §5). A participant bound to a seat of a formed coalition gets "Copy a follow link" on its home (#456). It makes a random 32-byte seed; the follow mailbox id and key derive from it through the same HKDF, with infos `reconcile/v1/follow-mailbox` and `reconcile/v1/follow-key` (`src/crypto/follow.ts`, pinned by `src/crypto/vectors/follow.json`, computed outside @noble), and it PUTs an M2-03 envelope there, `ttl_days=1`: a header `{secret, preview: {name, groups, excerpt}, chunkCount}`, exactly those keys (the coalition secret, its name, its member groups' names and its first section as plain text, each cut as an invite's preview is), then the coalition's relay-accepted log in chunks, as an invite packs a group's, with no entry marked `kept` (#269): a coalition's log takes no result, so a link with a marked entry reads as damaged, as a card does. Never `inner` or a seat secret: a header with any other key reads as damaged. Only once every PUT succeeds is the link, `${origin}/#follow/${seed}`, copied and shown, beside "Anyone who opens this link in the next 2 days can read this coalition from then on, including the names its participants chose and which group each comes from. They won't see inside any member group." The link, or the link bar (#444, `linkKind`), opens Follow, which takes the seed out of the address bar, opens the envelope and folds it in memory: a link that isn't a formed coalition's reads as expired or damaged, and one this device already reads merges in what it lacks. The preview shows as plain text inside `<bdi>`, then what following shows of the person, which is nothing, and Follow. Follow keeps a coalition entry marked `following`, imports the log through sync's verify, dedupe and fold path, and from then on the sync loop reads its mailboxes as any coalition's. A follower posts nothing, so neither the coalition's log nor the relay records that they follow, and followers never see each other. They read at the Member level (`FOLLOWER_LEVEL`, `followerCan` in `capabilities.ts`): the coalition's home shows a follower its member groups under its name, then its Dispatches, Documents and Decided list, with no proposing or Take part, and Unfollow, which purges the entry as Leave purges a group and posts nothing. Start lists each followed coalition under "Following". A followed entry goes in the backup file and a second device's link bundle with `following: true`. Every coalition home lists its Dispatches, newest first. Push for followers stays deferred, since #284 wakes member groups only: nothing posts a wake at a coalition Dispatch's minute, and a follower's endpoint under the coalition's mailboxes beside their own groups' would let the relay link them.
- **The room** (#125, ADR-0011, ADR-0009; `src/engine/room.ts`, `src/store/rooms.ts`, `src/screens/CoalitionRoom.tsx`). One small, end-to-end encrypted room per coalition, at `#/c/{coalitionId}/room`, under the room keys (§3): a log of its own, apart from the coalition's. A message is a signed record `{v: 1, type: "message", group: coalitionId, author, ts, body: {text}}`, `body` exactly `{text}`; the fold of a group's or coalition's own log ignores one. `foldRoom(records, coalition, relayNow)` shows, in relay order, each message once whose author is a current participant bound to one of the coalition's seats (so a participant who leaves takes theirs with them), whose text is 1–2,000 code points after NFC and trimming, and that was received at most 43,200 minutes (30 days) of relay time before `relayNow` (`vectors/room-*.json`). A device keeps one room entry (`kind: "room"`) for each coalition, not declined, whose seat a group it goes on in holds (not leaving or read-only), itself or through a coalition it is in, up to three levels (#344), with `inner` as its secret and the coalition entry's signing key and author; `keepRooms` makes it, and purges it, messages and outbox with it, on every pass from the store alone, offline too, once no such group is left, and remakes it if the coalition entry's key changed. The sync loop syncs each room after the coalitions as a group's, reading a never-read room's last 5 weeks, with no `kx`, chat bot, nudges, key changes or dissolve; each PUT asks `ttl_days=30` and wakes no one (push for room messages is deferred, below), and each pull deletes every stored room record received more than 43,200 minutes before its `now`. A sent message leaves the room's outbox only in the store change that saves the pull reading it back (#343), so the room lists it once throughout, from its outbox and then from its log; a pass that fails between the PUT and that pull posts it again, and dedupe by event id keeps one. The coalition home offers "Open the room" only on such a device. Bound participants write; someone in a member group who holds `propose` and hasn't joined gets Take part there first; anyone else in a member group reads. A device in no member group, as a follower's, gets no room. Deferred past the MVP: threads per Proposal, the "argument concluded" message, reactions, edits, deletion by the author, attachments, Markdown, mentions, read receipts, push for room messages, and a new room key when someone leaves a member group.
- **A group asking in, and the room** (#313). The room is for seated groups. A group asking into a formed coalition brings a card without `inner`, so while the coalition decides on it, its devices hold no key that opens a room message, those written before the card or during the vote included. Once its pass is final and it takes its seat, each participant from a seated group whose device holds the `inner` the genesis commits to, when their device next syncs, wraps it to its seat (`room-wrap`), and its devices open the first wrap that holds that `inner` (`innerFor`, #371): the room appears on its coalition home and reads its last 30 days, its own admission discussion included. A group the coalition doesn't admit never gets a wrap. Two other designs were weighed: a room key from `inner` and an epoch that moves on at each admission protects nothing, since the candidate holds `inner` and can derive the epoch in which it is discussed; and keeping `inner` on the card, with its warning naming the room, leaves the gap open. A seated participant could wrap a room of their own, and an admitted group that took the first wrap that opened would read it, cut off from the real room (#371). So the genesis commits to `inner` and a device takes only a wrap of it (§3 Room wraps; ADR-0075's 2026-11-12 amendment), and each participant wraps for themselves, whoever else from their seat has, since no one but the admitted group can open a wrap to see what it holds: any one honest participant's wrap repairs a wrong one. A new room key at each admission, which would also keep the admission discussion from the admitted group, and #129's seat secrets sealed to voting members only, are deferred: a pilot coalition asking for either is the trigger.
- **Nesting** (#122; ADR-0036, ADR-0074). A coalition reconciles with another coalition, or asks into one, through the same card one level up, and its groups decide that group by group. Seats carry `level`, 0 or none for a group's, and a coalition is 1 above its highest seat; the fold ignores a genesis, an admit motion or a reconcile target that would put a coalition above level 3, judged by the stated levels, and never reads another coalition's log to check one. Each founding seat's own result on a genesis is `fail` when the genesis states its seat at another level than its own.
  - **A coalition takes a seat.** "Reconcile with another coalition" on a coalition's row (`#/c/{coalitionId}/reconcile`), or Bring a card into a coalition this person takes part in, opens a motion in it with a reconcile target whose `secret`, `inner` and `seat` travel in one `sealed` field: the Seal under HKDF(its `inner`, `reconcile/v1/inner-seal`), with its id as associated data (`crypto/innerSeal.ts`, pinned by `crypto/vectors/innerSeal.json`), beside `key`, the public seat key, and the readable name and statement. Asking into a formed coalition, whose card carries no `inner` (#313), the field holds only `secret ‖ seat`, 64 bytes in the same one blob; once the coalition admits it, its devices open `inner` from a wrap for its seat, as an admitted group's do, and only then reach the coalitions above it. A coalition's log ignores a reconcile target that carries its secrets readable, one whose key is one of its own seats, and one from a coalition at level 3; a group's log ignores a sealed one. Its seats decide it as any motion; a card's seat and a genesis state the coalition's level. If the coalition above has formed, the admit step follows its pass, stating its level and its member groups' names (`groups`), which the ballot shows. The fold ignores an admit whose seat key is a seat here or this coalition's own seat key above, and a reconcile motion carrying a Dispatch, as it ignores an admit carrying one (#124). A seated participant whose device holds `inner`, a coalition's included, wraps it to each admitted seat, a coalition's included (§4 A group asking in, and the room), whose devices take only a wrap of the `inner` the genesis above commits to, as a group's do (#371); a coalition's room opens through a coalition's seat too (#344): a device in its groups opens the coalition's own room, then the sealed reconcile motion that holds its seat above, and takes the room above's `inner` from that motion, as a founding seat's does, or from a wrap for the seat once it is seated, so a coalition asking in reads nothing of the room above before or during its vote, and reads and writes it once seated, as an admitted group does. Only devices in a seated coalition's groups get it, and nothing new reaches the relay: they read the room's mailboxes as any member does. Any participant whose device opens `sealed` acts for the seat: it joins, reports and proposes the admission as a group's device does. The fold can't open `sealed`, so it takes the motion's `key` as stated; a device that opens it acts only when the seat secret inside makes that `key` (#375). One that doesn't is no seat to act for: a device that can open it shows no ballot on the motion, saying why, and none joins the coalition above under it, reports there for it or proposes its admission, so a hand-made motion can't give the seat above to a key nobody holds.
  - **A coalition seated above keeps its level** (#549; ADR-0036's 2026-11-21 amendment). While a coalition sits in a coalition above, or is on its way to a seat there (one of its reconcile motions hasn't failed: open, or passed; a fail, a lapse included, leaves it seated nowhere), it refuses any admission that would raise its own level (`raisesLevel`: the seat's stated level is at or above its own). Its seat above keeps the level it stated, so a level higher its results there would arrive as they stop counting (#387). The fold checks twice: an admit motion that would raise it is ignored when it opens (`mayAdmit`'s `seated`), and one that opened before the coalition's motion above, whose pass becomes final while it sits there, is refused at its horizon (`seatOrRefuse` in `fold.ts`): it seats nobody, `refusedAdmits` records its id, and it reads as a final fail, never as a pass that seats nobody. A group's seat, at level 0, never raises a coalition's level, so a group is never refused. Only the coalition's own log is read. On Bring a card, a coalition this person takes part in that its card's coalition would refuse isn't offered; it reads "<it> can't take this card. <coalition> sits in <above>. Admitting <it> would put <coalition> a level higher than its seat there, so its votes there would arrive too late to count. <it> can ask into <above> directly instead: ask a group in <above> for its card." (`refusedBy` and `seatAbove` in `crossing.ts`; `FoundCard.refused`). A coalition that sits in the coalition of the card isn't offered either: a coalition can't sit in one inside it. An admission refused at the final step reads, on group home under the coalition asking in, as "Didn't form" with the same why and route. The names are those the device already holds in the two coalitions' logs; nothing new reaches the relay. Pinned by `nested-levels.json`'s `forgery-admit-raises-seated-level`, `admit-raises-seated-level-at-final` and `admit-raises-unseated-level`, and the fast-check property that no admission ever raises the level of a coalition seated above.
  - **Motions down, results up.** `motionsFor(lower, seat)` also gives a coalition's outside motions, each with its `chain` (the coalitions further up, nearest first), so a motion opens in every group below, with the top coalition's deadline and bar; a group's Proposal names the coalition directly above as `coalition`. A coalition opens an outside motion once formed, with a snapshot of its seats and no ballots, and decides it from its seats' results as its own, at the level it holds, so its horizon is a day after that of each seat beneath it; it reports its outcome only once final, a fail at once and a pass at its own horizon, a day before results stop counting above (#387). Each log's result goes only to the coalition directly above it (`resultsToReport`), and `foldTogether` folds a device's logs in that order.
  - **Staggered horizons: only final outcomes cross** (#387; ADR-0036's 2026-11-14 amendment). Each level's results stop counting a day before the level above's: a motion with deadline `D` is final in a level-`k` coalition at `D + k·1,440`, a coalition being one level above every seat it holds. A coalition crosses a pass only once it is final, at its own horizon, and a fail at once, as a group crosses its result once its vote is final; so whatever arrives at any time in `[horizon(seat), horizon(above))` counts above. By induction from the groups up, each level's outcome depends only on the groups' final outcomes, never on the order or timing in which devices sync. #375's withdrawn-pass rule, under which a coalition answered a pass recorded for its seat that it didn't make with a fail at once, is gone: a coalition never crosses a pass that isn't final, and a pass for its seat forged by a holder of its seat secret is answered by its own final result, a fail making the seat contested, as for a group's seat.
  - **Store and screens.** The store keeps an entry for every coalition reachable upward from the device's live groups (`coalitionKeys`), opening each sealed seat under the `inner` a lower log holds; a follower, holding a secret but no `inner`, opens none and keeps no entry above. A dissolved group or a removed member's copy holds none (`ensureEntries`). Group home's coalition row lists the coalitions above it with their chain ("Riverside Tenants · in Eastside Housing · in Metro Renters"); a motion's tag names its route ("Coalition vote · Metro Renters (through Eastside Housing)"), and its progress reads each coalition on the way; a coalition's home marks a member coalition, and "Your coalition".
  - Golden vectors: `vectors/nested-*.json` (`{kind: "nested"}`), `nested-contest.json` among them (#375: a forged early pass a contest turns into a fail) and `nested-horizons.json` (#387: each level's horizon, a minute before it and at it, #375's five-seat case and its recovery, a forged early pass, and `formsFinal`'s close-out at level 2), and fast-check properties that a motion's outcome doesn't depend on the order lower results arrive in, that only a pass or fail for one motion and seat crosses each line, and that every level of three decides the same, the outcome each level's final results give, whatever the order and timing of results and crossings, at any seat count, with #375's five-seat counterexample and its recovery as examples. Deferred past the MVP: nesting deeper than 3 levels, a coalition leaving another, and reaching a higher coalition's room or followers from below.
- **Promotion** (#126; [ADR-0037](../../../adr/0037-holonic-promotion.md), ADR-0071, ADR-0065, ADR-0075 §1). A Dispatch rises one level at a time, each level deciding under its own rule.
  - **The record.** When a level passes a Dispatch whose ceiling leaves levels above it (a group's Dispatch put to its vote, once its pass is final, or a coalition's promotion motion, once its pass is final, at its horizon, #387), a participant bound to that level's seat in each coalition directly above posts `proposal {target: {kind: "promote", dispatch: {id, title, text}, ceiling, level, from}, title, why}`: the Dispatch's id, title and text, its ceiling, `level` the lines it has crossed (1 in the coalition above the group it started in), and `from` that seat (`promotionsOwed` in `crossing.ts`; the store's `settleCoalitions` posts it), until a day after the level below decided it: a group's close, or a coalition's horizon, plus 1,440 minutes. `title` is the Dispatch's and `why` is `""`; a link, window or Dispatch of its own is a bad shape, and no `doc` goes up. Nothing rises past the ceiling, past level 3, or from a coalition with nothing above it. A record restates only that, never the level below's record, so it is the same size at every level: at most about 4.5 KB at the Dispatch's limits, far under a record's 64,000 bytes (#377, pinned by `src/app/dispatchSize.test.ts`), and so it runs no size check.
  - **The coalition takes it** as a motion once formed, only from a current participant bound to `from`, one of its seats, with a level that fits that seat (1 from a group's; from a coalition's, 2 up to one above its stated level) and the ceiling, and words that fit a Dispatch's limits (`mayPromote`). The first per Dispatch id wins. It is ordinary, for the default window, with one version and no text, and every seat in its snapshot decides it, `from`'s included.
  - **No new vote below `from`.** `motionsFor` leaves a promotion motion out for the seat it came from, so it opens in every group beneath the coalition but `from`'s. `from`'s result comes from its own fold, as an ordinary `group-result`: `pass` only when it holds a passed Dispatch with that id, title, text, ceiling and level; `fail` for one it doesn't hold, one that says anything else, or one that didn't pass; nothing while it is still deciding. So a forged or altered record fails.
  - **Going out.** Each level that passes it sends it out on its Dispatches list: a group at its close, as above; a coalition once its pass is final, at its horizon, under the Dispatch's id, from the motion, pointing to no Document. A coalition home lists them among its Dispatches (#124), each "From <seat>". Its push is the group's result wake; a coalition's wakes no one, since a coalition's final pass takes no nudge and a coalition's mailboxes take no wake (#284, Push above).
  - **No re-votes** (`store/carry.ts`). A person's device keeps the score they gave a Dispatch that may rise on that group's record (its id, ceiling, the score and the ballot's minute, never the note), until it reaches its ceiling or for 30 days after the last decision on it the device has seen; never in a backup file or a link. When a promotion motion for it opens in another group on the device where they are eligible and haven't voted, the device draws a relay minute uniformly in the rest of the window (48 random bits from `crypto/random.ts`), keeps it, and casts the same score under their identity there at the first pass at or after it, before the deadline, with fresh salt and no note. The ballot says "Your 3 from another group will be cast here before voting ends.", with Change and Don't cast. A carried 0 is never cast on its own: the ballot asks "You gave this a 0 elsewhere. Write a fresh reason for this group.", and refuses a note the person wrote on that Dispatch in another group (case, spacing and Unicode form aside), since identical words would link the two identities.
  - Golden vectors: `vectors/promote-*.json` (`promote-dispatch.json` a fold file; the others nested, with `promote` matched against `promotionsOwed`), and fast-check properties that a record carries exactly its Dispatch's id, title and text, ceiling, level and seat, and nothing of the group below, and that a seat passes only the record it holds exactly. Deferred past the MVP: lowering a ceiling once voting starts, and carrying a section into a higher coalition's Documents.
- **Deferred past the MVP:** changing the threshold, competing versions on a motion, a group leaving a coalition, coalitions across relays (ordering by seq relies on one relay's counter), and new coalition secrets when someone leaves a member group (ADR-0074, ADR-0075 §1).

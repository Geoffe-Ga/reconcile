### Dispatches

#112 builds them in `client/src/engine/dispatches.ts` and `fold.ts` ([ADR-0003](../../../adr/0003-three-tier-involvement-levels.md), [ADR-0012](../../../adr/0012-content-free-push.md), ADR-0074). A Dispatch is news for the whole group: a title, a short plain text and, optionally, a Document it points to. A Co-Creator sends one, or a Proposal carries one that goes out when it passes ("Updated: Mission statement"). Every level follows the group and reads its Dispatches; the Dispatch's text lives only in the sealed group log, and the push it rides says only "Something new in Reconcile" (§7, Push). Scheduled weekly or monthly Dispatches, the digest, targeting particular people, "new" dots, and editing or deleting a Dispatch stay deferred (ADR-0074, §12).

| Type | Body | Valid when | Built by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dispatch` | `{title, text, doc?}`: the title, the text, and the id of a Document it points to | the author holds `dispatch` (`can()`: a Co-Creator holding voting rights who isn't suspended); the title is 1–80 code points and the text at most 500 (`dispatchFits`); and `doc`, when present, is the id of one of the group's Documents as the Dispatch finds them (`documentIds`: `state.documents`, one whose sections were all removed included, and a new Document only once its pass is final, #92) | #112 |

- **Carried by a Proposal.** Any `proposal`, an edit or a targeted one (§4, Proposals and ballots), may carry `dispatch: {title, text}` with the same limits; a `proposal` whose `dispatch` breaks them is ignored whole, and any other key inside it, a `doc` included, is dropped. The folded Proposal keeps it as `dispatch`, or null. When its pass is final, the fold appends a Dispatch with `id` and `by: {proposal: id}` set to the Proposal's id, `doc` set to the Document it changed or created (`documentOf`: its Atom's Document for an edit, a removal included, the Document an add names, #75, and a new Document's own id, #92; null for a steward, rules or dissolve Proposal), and `received` set to its close time (`closedAt`, or else the deadline). A fail or a lapse sends nothing, nor does a Proposal still open when its group dissolves (#108). Its push is the result's wake (§7, Push).
- **Order.** `state.dispatches` keeps each Dispatch as `{id, by, title, text, doc, received}`, with `doc` null when there is none, in the order they went out: a sent one at its receipt, in relay order, and the ones passed Proposals carry, brought up to `at` before each record like the steward seats, in close order (close time, then the Proposal's relay order), ahead of every record received from their close on. So `received` never goes down along the list. The Dispatches view lists them newest first (`newestFirst`).
- **Lengths** count code points as received, like every other limit: the author's device normalizes to NFC before signing, and the fold never re-normalizes (§4, Envelope). The screens count with `nfcLength` before sending.
- **Rising by vote** (#126; [ADR-0037](../../../adr/0037-holonic-promotion.md), [ADR-0071](../../../adr/0071-self-hosting-and-reach.md)). Once the group sits in a formed coalition, Send a Dispatch asks "How far can it go?": This group only (a `dispatch`, sent at once, as above), One level up, Two levels up, or As far as the coalitions go. Any choice above this group sends a `proposal` with `target: {kind: "dispatch", ceiling, doc?}` and the Dispatch as its `dispatch`, titled as the Dispatch with an empty why; `ceiling` is `1`, `2` or `"coalitions"`, and nothing above coalitions. It is valid when its author may send that Dispatch now (`maySendDispatch`, `doc` included) and any `window` fits the ordinary class. It is ordinary (`targetClass`), changes no Document, has one version under its own id with no text, so the fold ignores a `version` on it, and its ceiling can't change once it opens. When its pass is final it goes out as any carried Dispatch does, pointing to its `doc`, which stays in this group. Its id is the Dispatch's id at every level it reaches. How it rises is under Coalitions, Promotion. Like a `dispatch`, it runs no size check before it is queued: at the composer's limits (80 code points of title, which it carries twice, 500 of text, each at most 6 bytes as JSON, and one of the group's own Document ids), its record is under 4.5 KB, far below the 64,000 bytes a record may take (#377, pinned by `src/app/dispatchSize.test.ts`). A record over that would be refused by the relay and hold the group's outbox, since an entry leaves it only once a pull reads it back (#369).

### Dissolution

#108 builds it in `client/src/engine/dissolve.ts` and `fold.ts` ([ADR-0051](../../../adr/0051-dissolution-and-rule-classes.md), [ADR-0072](../../../adr/0072-protocol-details-for-decomposition.md) §10, [ADR-0075](../../../adr/0075-safeguards-for-the-revised-mvp.md) §5). The fold and the screens ask the same functions there (`mayProposeDissolve`, `dissolveDue`, `closedByDissolve`), so a screen never offers what the fold would ignore.

| Type | Body | Valid when | Built by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `proposal` with a dissolve target | `{target: {kind: "dissolve", wipe}, title, why, link?, window?}`: `wipe` is `true` or `false` (anything else is a bad shape), and whether every device also erases the group. Any `text` is dropped, and an `atom` makes it a bad shape | the group hasn't dissolved; the author holds `propose`; no other dissolve Proposal is open; the title is 1–120 code points and the justification at most 2,000; and any `window` passes `validWindow` for the entrenched class: 14 to 28 days, default 14 | #108 |
| `dissolve` | `{proposal}`: the dissolve Proposal whose pass is final; any other field is dropped | the group hasn't dissolved; the author is a member (`can(author, 'read')`), a newcomer still earning a vote included (#101); and `proposal` names a dissolve Proposal that, at this event's receipt, has closed (early, or at its deadline) with a pass at the entrenched bar | #108 |

- **The vote.** A dissolve Proposal is entrenched (§5's `{3, 5}` bar, `rules.windows.entrenched`), opens with one version under its own id and no text, takes no `version` events, and has everyone `wouldVote` lets in, so holding voting rights at a level that votes (#101), in its eligible set. Competing dissolve versions stay deferred.
- **The event.** Any member's device that folds the final pass, while no `dissolve` has folded, posts one (`settleDissolve`, §7). The fold takes the first valid one and sets `state.dissolved = {received, wipe}`.
- **After it.** The fold ignores every later event, a second `dissolve` included, and relay time stops at its receipt: `at` stays there, and so does the minute every Proposal is settled at. A Proposal still open then never closes: it applies no text, seats nobody, and has no outcome (`final` stays false), and the screens read "Closed when the group dissolved". No `vouch` lands, and a newcomer's wait still running then never ends: a `rightsAt` later than the dissolve's receipt never arrives, so they never hold rights (#101). Golden vectors: `vectors/dissolve-*.json`.

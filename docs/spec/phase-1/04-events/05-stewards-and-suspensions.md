### Stewards and suspensions

#102 builds them in `client/src/engine/stewards.ts` ([ADR-0016](../../../adr/0016-suspend-then-vote.md), [ADR-0049](../../../adr/0049-stewards-and-removal.md), [ADR-0059](../../../adr/0059-suspension-during-removal.md), [ADR-0072](../../../adr/0072-protocol-details-for-decomposition.md) §4, [ADR-0075](../../../adr/0075-safeguards-for-the-revised-mvp.md) §3). The fold and the screens ask the same functions there (`mayProposeSteward`, `maySuspend`, `electionsDue`), so a screen never offers what the fold would ignore.

- **Seats.** `state.stewards` holds seat 1 and seat 2 as `{seat, holder, termEnd, elected}`. The genesis author holds seat 1 with no term end (`termEnd: null`) until an election passes. "Voting members" are counted by `votingRightsCount(state)` in `capabilities.ts`: the people holding voting rights, whatever level they chose, ignoring suspensions (ADR-0075 §3, #101).
- **Elections and recalls.** A steward Proposal is ordinary, opens with one version under its own id and no text, and takes no `version` events. A recall's eligible set leaves out the steward it would remove, or their own 0 alone would block it in a group of 10 or fewer. The folded target also records `holder`, who held the seat when it opened.
- **Seating.** The fold applies a steward Proposal once it closes, in order of close time, then relay order. A pass seats the nominee for a term ending `stewards.termDays` × 1,440 minutes after the close (262,080), but seat 2's first-ever term is 91 days (131,040), so the seats come due about three months apart. The nominee is seated only if their level still votes and they don't hold the other seat; a suspension doesn't stop it, so a steward can't block an election by suspending the nominee. A recall's pass empties the seat if the steward it named still holds it.
- **Emptying a seat.** A seat empties when its holder steps down to a level that doesn't vote, on a recall, and, through `vacate` in `stewards.ts`, on the holder's `leave` (#77) and `remove` (#115). Past its term end a holder keeps the seat until a successor is seated; seat 2 can seat none below 6 voting members, so in a group that has shrunk below 6 it empties at its term end. `electionsDue(state)` lists the seats whose election could open now and that are empty, held by the founder before the first election, or past their term end.
- **Suspensions.** `state.suspensions` keeps every suspension in relay order as `{id, seq, received, by, member, reason, until, removal}`. One runs from its `received` until `until`, `received` + `suspensionDays` × 1,440 (10,080 minutes), unless a removal vote extends it (`removal`, Removal below). Meanwhile `can()` gives the member only `read` and `see-roster`, and the fold ignores their events (§4, The fold) apart from their `leave`, since exit is always possible (ADR-0010, #77). Ballots received before it still count, and a later ballot doesn't replace them. They stay in the eligible set of Proposals that open during it (`wouldVote` ignores suspensions), so a suspension never shrinks the electorate, and those Proposals can't close early without them. Stewards can't lengthen or lift one; a removal vote against the member, opened while it runs, is the only thing that extends it (ADR-0059, #115). The reason stays in the signed log; expiring it stays deferred (ADR-0074).

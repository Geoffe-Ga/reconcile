### Opt-ins

#117 builds them in `client/src/engine/optIns.ts`, with the steward's narrowing in `stewards.ts` ([ADR-0038](../../../adr/0038-optional-discovery.md), [ADR-0039](../../../adr/0039-integrations-per-group-opt-in.md), [ADR-0040](../../../adr/0040-group-opt-ins-are-governed.md), ADR-0074, ADR-0075 §3 and §5). A group has two opt-ins, both off at founding: the Discord chat bot (`chatBot`: `null`, or `{webhook}`), which posts content-free nudges, ADR-0039's step 1 (#119, Chat bot below); and the front door (`frontDoor`: `false`, or `{sign, box, showCount}` once open, with `listed: true` and `covenant` once listed, #485), whose page #121 builds (Front door, below). Each changes only by a rules Proposal, never by a toggle. The screens ask the same functions the fold does (`mayProposeRules`, `mayTurnOffNow`), so a screen never offers what the fold would ignore.

| Type | Body | Valid when | Built by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `proposal` with a rules target | `{target: {kind: "rules", optIn, value}, title, why, link?, window?}`: the opt-in and the value it would set, kept as written, and the voting window in whole days (#106) | the author holds `propose`; the title is 1–120 code points and the justification at most 2,000; `optIn` is `chatBot` or `frontDoor`; `value` passes that opt-in's validator and differs from its standing value; no other rules Proposal for that opt-in is open; and `window`, when present, is within `windows[class]` for the class `targetClass` gives it (14 to 28 days to turn one on, 3 to 14 to turn one off). The chat bot's validator takes `null` or `{webhook}` with no other field, where the webhook is the whole string `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/<digits>/<token>` (or `discordapp.com`), with a token of letters, digits, `_` and `-`, and no port, user, query, fragment or capital in the host. It is one pattern over the string, never a URL parser, so every browser folds the same answer. The front door's takes `false`, or exactly `{sign, box, showCount}`: two 32-byte secrets in canonical unpadded base64url and a boolean, with no other field (#121), optionally with `listed: false`, or with `listed: true` and `covenant`, a SHA-256 in canonical unpadded base64url: `covenant` is there exactly when `listed` is true (#485). The covenant is checked for its shape alone, never against the texts a build carries, so a build without a version's words folds the vote on it the same | #117 |
| `narrow` | `{optIn, proposal}`: the opt-in, and the author's own off-Proposal for it | `can(author, 'narrow')`; no recall of the author is open; the opt-in isn't off in effect; `proposal` is an open rules Proposal for that opt-in, setting it to off (`null` or `false`), by the same author; and no off-Proposal for that opt-in failed or lapsed within the last 30 × 1,440 minutes: a `narrow` received 43,200 minutes or fewer after such a close is ignored | #117 |

- **Standing value.** `state.rules.optIns` holds each opt-in's standing value: the founding default (a genesis can't open one, §11), then the value of the last rules Proposal for it that passed, applied once it closes, in order of close time, then relay order, as the steward seats are. A membership's end sets the front door's to `false`, and a passed front door Proposal it exposed sets nothing (Front door, below; #288).
- **Class.** `isWider(optIn, from, to)` is true from off to on, and for the front door from hiding its count to showing it (#121) and from unlisted to listed (#485), each whatever else changes, so turning an opt-in on, showing the count or listing the door is entrenched (a window of 14 to 28 days, 14 by default, and the entrenched bar), and turning it off, replacing one webhook with another, rotating a front door's keys, hiding its count, unlisting it or accepting another covenant version is ordinary (3 to 14 days, 7 by default, and the ordinary bar; ADR-0075 §5). `targetClass` reads the standing value when the Proposal opens, and the fold records it as the target's `from`, so a proposer can't understate the change. A rules Proposal opens with one version under its own id and no text, and takes no `version` events.
- **Narrowing.** `state.narrowings` keeps every steward narrowing in relay order as `{id, seq, received, by, optIn, proposal}`. From the `narrow`'s receipt, the opt-in is off until its off-Proposal closes: off for good if that passes, and back to the standing value at its close if it fails or lapses (ADR-0040, ADR-0075 §3). Every member reads it: Group rules names the steward who turned it off, and the off-Proposal's ballot says so too.
- **Effective value.** `effectiveOptIn(state, optIn)` is off while a narrowing of it is in force and the standing value otherwise; `state.optIns` holds each one's effective value at `at`. The chat bot (#119) and the front door (#121) read only the effective value.
- **Listing** (#485; [ADR-0081](../../../adr/0081-listed-doors-and-exception-moderation.md), [`listed-doors.md`](../../listed-doors.md) §3). A listed door's `covenant` is the SHA-256 of the UTF-8 covenant text its voters read (`client/src/shelf/covenant.ts`: versioned, each version's hash pinned by a test, the pilot draft marked a draft wherever it shows). One Proposal may open and list a door, or list one already open. Unlisting writes the door as before #485, `{sign, box, showCount}`, and every build reads `listed: false` as that same door. The listing is part of the door, so it ends whenever the door closes: by a vote, by a steward's narrowing, or at a membership's end (#288). `listingInEffect(state)` (`frontDoor.ts`) is the covenant the door is listed under in effect, or null. Relisting after a close is a new opening Proposal with fresh secrets. Nothing is posted to the shelf yet. Golden vectors: `listed-door.json`.
- **Older apps** (#485). A build from before #485 refuses a listed value, so it ignores a listing Proposal and the votes on it, and folds the same once it updates; every device in a group runs a build with #485 before that group's first listing Proposal. The reasoning and the options weighed are in [`listed-doors.md`](../../listed-doors.md) §3.1.
- **The webhook is a credential.** Anyone holding it can post into the channel. It exists only inside sealed group events and in folded state on members' devices, never readable at the relay, and screens show only its last 4 characters (§9).

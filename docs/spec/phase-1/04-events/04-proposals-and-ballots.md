### Proposals and ballots

- **Versions** ([ADR-0004](../../../adr/0004-competing-versions-go-to-ballot.md), [ADR-0047](../../../adr/0047-ballot-cap-quorum-reproposals.md), #72). A Proposal's own text is version 1, and its id is the Proposal's id. A `version` event's id is its own event id. Versions keep relay order, which the winner's tie rule uses, whatever their `ts`. A ballot holds at most 3 versions (`VERSIONS_MAX`).
- **One open Proposal per Atom** (ADR-0073, #72). A Proposal is open from its receipt until its deadline or its early close, whichever comes first, both in relay order: an event received in the minute of an early close, after the ballot that closed it, finds it closed, and one before it finds it open, on every device (Results, The closing minute, below). The fold ignores a `proposal` on an Atom that has an open one, and new wording joins that one as a version instead. `composeOption(state, atomId)` (`client/src/engine/composeOption.ts`) gives the screens the same rule over folded state: `'propose'` when nothing is open on the Atom, `'add-version'` while its open ballot has room, `'ballot-full'` at 3 versions, `'moving'` while a move of it is open (#103), and `'protecting'` while a class change of it is open (#116), which take nothing more: a move or a class change holds its section's slot as an edit does, so a change, a removal, a move, a class change or a version of it is ignored, and a section's class can't change while any Proposal on it is open. It and the fold share `optionFor`, so the composer never offers what the fold would ignore.
- **Targets** (#75, #92, #102, #107, #108, #115, #116, #117). An edit names an `atom`. An add carries `target: {kind: "add", document, after: <atom id> | null}` instead, where `null` means the start of that Document, and the new Atom's id is the Proposal's id, in that Document; a non-null anchor must be in the Document the add names when it's made, so one in another Document makes the add invalid. A move carries `target: {kind: "move", atom, after: <atom id> | null}` and no `text` (#103): its folded `atom` is the section it moves, so it holds that section's slot, its `text` is `""`, and its ballot is one version, under its own id, with no text, which takes no others; the anchor must be another Atom of the section's own Document when the move is made (moving sections between Documents, moving a header with the sections under it, drag and drop and outline numbering stay deferred). A class change carries `target: {kind: "class", atom, to}` and no `text` (#116): like a move, its folded `atom` is the section it changes, so it holds that section's slot, its `text` is `""`, and its ballot is one version, under its own id, with no text, which takes no others. Setting a section's class when it's founded or added, the Reconcile-wide eternal core ([ADR-0035](../../../adr/0035-reconcile-group-minimal-eternal-core.md)) and the part-ways petition an entrenched change can trigger stay deferred. A new Document carries `target: {kind: "new-document", title}`, and its `text` holds its first sections: it names no Atom, so the one-open-Proposal-per-Atom rule doesn't apply and several can be open at once, its versions compete on the text while the title stays fixed, and the folded Proposal's `atom` is its own id, the Document it would create, so Add your version gathers under it as an add's does. An edit whose version text is `""` removes the Atom; only an edit can carry `""`, so a section's removal and rewordings of one Atom compete on one ballot. Every rule that reads the Document reads it as the event finds it: the genesis Atoms with every pass that is final by the event's relay minute applied, in pass order. So a Proposal on an Atom that a pass has removed, or that an add hasn't created yet, is ignored, and so is an add whose anchor isn't in the Document. A steward Proposal carries `target: {kind: "steward", seat, member}` and no `atom` or `text` (Stewards and suspensions, below), and so does a rules Proposal, with `target: {kind: "rules", optIn, value}` (Opt-ins, below). A dissolve Proposal carries `target: {kind: "dissolve", wipe}` (Dissolution, below), a member's removal `target: {kind: "remove", member}` (Removal, below), which removes a person, never a section, and a priority vote `target: {kind: "priority"}` and its `options` (Priority votes, below). The folded Proposal keeps its `target`: `{kind: "edit"}` for an edit, the add's for an add, whose `atom` is its own id, the steward change, the rules change `{kind: "rules", optIn, value, from}`, the dissolve target, the member's removal, or `{kind: "priority"}`; a steward, rules, dissolve or member removal Proposal's `atom` and `text` are `""`, so a member removal's one version's `""` removes no Atom.
- **Priority votes** (#107, ADR-0074, `client/src/engine/priority.ts`). A `proposal` whose target is `{kind: "priority"}` asks the group what to focus on: its `title` is the question, and its `options`, 2–7 in ballot order, are what everyone scores. Option `i` goes on the ballot as a version with the id `${proposalId}.${i}`, all by the proposer, so ballots, the eligible set, quorum per option, the bar (ordinary), early close and the window work as they do for versions; an optional `window` is checked as an edit's is (#106). The folded Proposal keeps `target`, its own id as its `atom` (no Atom has it) and `""` as its text. The author NFC-normalizes and trims each option before signing (`optionText`), and the fold checks them as received, never re-normalizing: each is 1–120 code points and not blank, and no two are equal. A group has at most one priority vote open at a time, in relay order, and the fold ignores a second one while the first is open, and any `version` on a priority vote. A priority vote changes no Atom. Like any of the group's own Proposals it takes the next `number` (#123), so the chat bot claims its moments and its first claimant records its Discord thread, and it may carry a `dispatch`, which goes out when its pass is final with `doc` null (#112; Dispatches, below). A suspended member's priority vote or ballot is ignored like any other event of theirs (#102), and so is one from a newcomer still earning a vote (#101). A coalition's log ignores a priority vote, as it does every targeted Proposal (§4 Coalitions, #100), and so does a group that has dissolved (#108). **The focus:** `state.focus` is `{proposal, option, text}` for the top option (`rank()[0]`, §5) of the last priority vote whose pass is final, in close order, or null. A priority vote that fails or lapses leaves it as it was, and so does one still open when the group dissolves, which never closes; the next one to pass replaces it. The vectors are `priority-rank.json`, `priority-options.json`, `priority-focus.json`, `priority-chat-thread.json`, `priority-dispatch.json`, `priority-dissolve.json`, `priority-suspend.json`, `priority-admission.json`, `priority-coalition.json` and `priority-cleared.json`. Like any Proposal's, its ballots and notes expire 30 days after it closes (#111): a cleared priority vote closes, counts and decides by its result, so its outcome and the focus survive clearing.
- **Eligibility** ([ADR-0015](../../../adr/0015-anti-capture.md), #77, #90, #101). The eligible voters are the members at the Proposal's place in relay order for whom `wouldVote` holds: people who joined before it and hadn't left, whose voting rights had arrived, at a level that grants `vote`. A later join, leave or level change never changes an open Proposal's eligible set, quorum or early close. A ballot also needs `vote` when the relay receives it, so someone who steps down to Member can't vote until they step back up, while the vote is open.
- **Class and deadline** (#94, #106). `targetClass(state, body)` in `client/src/engine/rules.ts` gives a Proposal its class, `ordinary` or `entrenched`, from the state when it opens. It never reads a field the proposer writes, so nobody can understate a change. The Proposal keeps it as `class`. `chosenWindow(rules, class, body.window)` gives the whole days its vote runs, kept as `window`: the proposer's `window`, or `windows[class].default` when there is none, and null, so the fold ignores the Proposal, for a window `validWindow` refuses. Its deadline is its `received` plus `window` × 1,440 minutes, fixed when it opens: 10,080 for an ordinary change with no window, 14,400 for a window of 10. Early close (below) still applies and doesn't move the deadline. Its versions are tallied at `rules.bars[class]` (§5). A rules Proposal that widens an opt-in from its standing value (`isWider`, Opt-ins below) is entrenched, so with no window it runs 20,160 minutes, its window must be 14 to 28 days, and it needs the entrenched bar (#117). A class change is entrenched whatever it asks, so entrenchment is itself entrenched, and so is a change, a removal or a move of an entrenched section (#116, ADR-0015 §2): `targetClass` reads the section's class from `state.atoms` when the Proposal opens, so it runs 14 to 28 days, 14 by default, and needs the entrenched bar (`vectors/classes-*.json`; the issue's example, an entrenched edit at an average of 2.75 with no zeros, fails where the ordinary bar would pass it). An add stays ordinary wherever its anchor, and so does a change of an eternal section, which the fold ignores anyway. Every other target is ordinary, steward elections and recalls included (ADR-0049). Dissolving the group is entrenched (#108). Removing a member has its own class, `removal` (#115): its own bar (§5) and a fixed window of 14 days, `windows.removal` (§11). A steward or removal Proposal carries no `window` and runs its class's default: 7 days for a steward Proposal, 14 for a removal.
- **Ballots.** A ballot scores versions by version id. A voter's latest valid ballot replaces their earlier one in full, so a version it leaves out loses that voter's score. A ballot that names a version not yet on the Proposal, in relay order, is ignored, even once that version arrives, and so is a ballot received after the deadline or the close. An ignored ballot never replaces the voter's earlier one.
- **Close.** A Proposal closes early at the first ballot after which every eligible member's latest ballot scores every version; `closedAt` is that ballot's `received`. Otherwise it closes once `relayNow` reaches the deadline. A new version restarts the wait for an early close: it closes only once every eligible member's latest ballot scores the new version too.
- **Apply.** Only a closed Proposal's outcome is final. A final pass sets the target Atom's text, removes it (`""`), or puts the add's winning text right after its anchor in its Document, first there for `null`, or at the end of its own Document if the anchor is gone by then. A move's pass takes its Atom out of its place and puts it right after its anchor the same way, first for `null`, or at the end of its Document once the anchor is gone (#103; `vectors/move-*.json`, and `properties.test.ts` shows passed moves keep every Atom id and its bytes, and follow close time, then relay order, whatever order their ballots arrive in). A class change's pass sets its Atom's `class` to `to` (#116), and an edit's pass keeps its Atom's class. A new Document's pass adds a Document after every other (#92): its id is the Proposal's, its title the target's, and its Atoms are `documentSections(winning text)` (`splitAtoms`, each ending in one LF) with ids `${proposalId}.${i}`. A fail or a lapse creates nothing, and a pass in one Document leaves every other Document's Atoms byte for byte (`properties.test.ts`). Final passes apply in order of close time (`closedAt`, the relay time of the ballot that completed the vote, or else the deadline), with ties broken by the Proposal's relay order, so every device builds the same Document. When two adds share an anchor, the one applied later ends up directly after it (`vectors/sections-*.json`).
- **Leave** (#77). After `leave`, `can()` refuses the author everything, so the fold ignores their later events, a join under the same key included. Ballots received before the leave still count, and the leaver's Proposals and versions stay on the ballot. An open Proposal keeps them in its eligible set, so its quorum and early close don't change, and a Proposal that opens later leaves them out. `applyLeave` in `client/src/engine/fold.ts` is the one place a leave takes effect, so whatever else a member holds, such as a steward's seat (#102), empties there. `displayName` names former members too, so their ballots and Proposals still say who. Golden vectors: `vectors/leave-*.json`.
- **Results** (#111, [ADR-0017](../../../adr/0017-need-to-see-and-expiry.md), [ADR-0065](../../../adr/0065-stand-aside-and-ballot-notes.md) §3). A `result` is a checkpoint, not a tally event: the fold still tallies from ballots whenever it holds them.
  - **Commitment.** `commit` is the unpadded base64url SHA-256 of `"reconcile/v1/ballot-commit" ‖ 0x00 ‖ utf8(JSON.stringify(entries))`, where `entries` lists each counted ballot as `[voterKey, scores in version order (null if unscored), salt]`, sorted by voter key in code-unit order (`client/src/engine/commit.ts`). A ballot without a salt enters with `""`. Notes never enter it, so a result never carries one. The salts mean nobody can test guessed ballots against it once the ballots are gone (`commit.test.ts` tries every assignment of a 4-voter result's counts).
  - **Accepted.** `fold(records, relayNow, outside, cleared)` keeps, on each Proposal, the first `result` in relay order that a current member wrote in a minute after `closedAt`, whose `eligible` and version ids are the Proposal's, and whose `outcome` follows from its own counts through `tally()` at the Proposal's bar (a coalition motion's bar is the one the coalition set, #100). While the device holds the ballots, its counts, `closedAt` and `commit` must also equal what they give (`heldRecord`), and so must its `closedSeq` when it names one: the seq of the Proposal's last ballot, the one that closed it, and none for a deadline close (#289). A result from before #289 names none and still counts. One from a member, received after the close, that doesn't is ignored and its author listed in the Proposal's `mismatched`. A result received before the close, or in its minute, is ignored and not listed. So "no ballots" means nobody voted, and a result claiming votes is rejected there.
  - **Cleared.** For a Proposal in `cleared`, the fold reads its first `result` after it in relay order ahead, before the fold starts, and checks it when the Proposal opens: it arrived in a later minute than its `closedAt`, closes the Proposal between its receipt and its deadline, names the Proposal's eligible count with its first version first (version 1, or a priority vote's first option, #107), counts every eligible member on every version for an early close (and no more than them otherwise), and names the outcome its counts give at the Proposal's bar. A `closedSeq`, when it names one, must be an early close's and come after the Proposal's seq and before the result's own. If it holds up, the Proposal counts and decides by it from the start, and closes where its ballots closed it: its `closedAt` stays null until the fold passes the record's `closedSeq` in relay order, so events before the closing ballot find it open, those after find it closed, and a steward pass seats its steward at the close, all as with the ballots (The closing minute, below). A record from before #289 names no `closedSeq`, so its Proposal closes at the start of its close's minute, and in that minute a version is on the ballot only if the result lists it. When the fold reaches the result, it keeps it as the Proposal's `result` if a current member wrote it and it lists the Proposal's versions; if not, the fold runs once more without reading it ahead. A Proposal with no record that holds up has no early close and lapses. Any ballot still in the log for it is ignored. A coalition motion opened from `outside` (#100) opens where `outside` puts it, so its read-ahead result is the first after that place. `properties.test.ts` shows that folding the full log and the cleared log give deep-equal Atoms and outcomes, and `expiry.test.ts` that a cleared steward election seats the same steward, that a cleared add or removal (#75) changes the Document as its ballots did, that a cleared Proposal carrying a Dispatch (#112) sends it at its close, that a cleared priority vote (#107) decides and sets the focus as its ballots did (`vectors/priority-cleared.json`), and that a group whose ballots on its reconcile Proposal and on a coalition motion are cleared owes and flags the same results for its seat (`resultsToReport`, `resultMismatches`, #100).
  - **The closing minute** (#289). An event received in the minute of an early close is judged by its place in relay order: before the closing ballot it finds the Proposal open, after it closed, on every device. A device that holds the ballots knows the place from them; one that has cleared them reads it from the result's `closedSeq`. A rule for the whole minute won't do. Judging it all closed can't be done by a device holding the ballots, which can't know an event falls in a closing minute until the closing ballot arrives, and a suspension or dissolve before that ballot could then undo the very close it was judged by. Judging it all open would undo #72's rule that an event after the closing ballot finds the Proposal closed. Every rule reads openness through `isOpenAt` (`client/src/engine/open.ts`): the fold, and the steward, opt-in and dissolve rules the screens share (`stewards.ts`, `optIns.ts`, `dissolve.ts`), which each checked `closedAt` themselves and so read a cleared Proposal as closed from its opening. The vectors `steward-cleared.json`, `optin-cleared.json`, `dissolve-cleared.json` and `result-closing-minute.json` are each folded with the ballots held and as a device that has cleared them (`alsoCleared`), and must agree, and `properties.test.ts` puts a Proposal on the same section anywhere in a closing minute.
  - **Where results go.** Only a group's own log takes a `result`: a coalition's log ignores one (#100), and a group that has dissolved takes no event after its dissolve (#108), so `resultsDue` names none for either. A result never crosses group lines: what a coalition sees of a group's vote stays its `group-result`, a pass or fail.
  - The vectors are `client/src/engine/vectors/result-*.json` and #289's four above, their commitments computed in Python.

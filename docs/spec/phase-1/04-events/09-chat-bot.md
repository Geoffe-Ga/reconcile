### Chat bot

#119 builds it: the moments and the claim rule in `client/src/engine/chatPosts.ts`, the posting in `client/src/store/chatBot.ts`, and the request in `client/src/net/discord.ts` (ADR-0039 step 1, ADR-0074). While a group has its bot on in effect (`effectiveOptIn(state, 'chatBot')`), its Discord channel hears of each Proposal, steward and rules Proposals included, three times. No server holds the webhook, so one member's device posts each notice, and the log decides which.

| Type | Body | Valid when | Built by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chat-posted` | `{proposal, moment, nonce?}`: the Proposal, the moment claimed (`open`, `closing` or `result`), and 16 random bytes in base64url that the fold doesn't read, so two devices holding one key (#78) never sign the same bytes | the author is a member who isn't suspended and whom no passed removal names (#115); the bot is on in effect at its receipt (neither off nor narrowed); `proposal` is one of the group's own Proposals in the fold, never a coalition motion (#100), which has no number for a thread's name; `moment` is the one due at its receipt (below); and no claim of that (Proposal, moment) was kept before it. The first valid claim wins, in relay order | #119 |
| `chat-thread` | `{proposal, url}`: the Proposal, and the link to the Discord forum thread its opening notice opened | the author is a member who isn't suspended, whom no passed removal names (#115), and who made the winning `open` claim for that Proposal, so no other member can point Join discussion at another server; the bot is on in effect at its receipt; `url` is the whole string `https://discord.com/channels/{server id}/{thread id}`, each id 17 to 20 digits (`^https://discord\.com/channels/\d{17,20}/\d{17,20}$`, one pattern over the string); and no thread was kept for that Proposal before it. The first valid one wins, in relay order | #123 |

- **Moments.** At any relay minute at most one moment of a Proposal is due (`dueMoment`). `open`: from its receipt until it closes or until `deadline − 1,440`, whichever comes first. `closing`: from `deadline − 1,440` until it closes. `result`: from its close (`closedAt`, or else `deadline`) until 1,440 minutes after. A vote that closes early before its last day skips `closing`. Because the fold checks a claim against the moment due at its receipt, nobody can claim a moment early to silence it, and turning the bot on or joining never replays an old result. There is no `version` notice.
- **Winning claims.** `state.chatPosts` keeps each winning claim in relay order as `{id, seq, received, by, proposal, moment}`. The fold and the devices ask the same function (`mayClaim`), so a device never makes a claim the fold would ignore.
- **Claim, then post** (`createChatBot`, run by the sync loop after each group syncs, §7). While the bot is on, the device enqueues a `chat-posted` for each moment due at the relay's `now` that has no winning claim and that it hasn't claimed already, and keeps the claim's id in the group's record (`chatClaims`). It posts a claim only when the winning claim's id is one it made and that moment is still due at `now`, settling the claim first in one store change, so two tabs can't both post it, and never retries. It drops a claim another claim beat, one the fold ignored, and one whose moment has passed. While a steward's narrowing holds the bot off, a winning claim waits, and goes out if the bot comes back on while its moment is still due. A device whose group is leaving (#77) claims and posts nothing, and so does one whose group has dissolved (#108): it posts no notice, not even the dissolve's result, and keeps no claim. A slow or failing Discord never holds up sync. A closing or result notice therefore goes out the next time any member's app syncs after that moment.
- **The post** (`postWebhook`). It checks the webhook with the opt-in's own validator and refuses anything else before `fetch` runs, rebuilds the URL as `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/{id}/{token}` (so a `discordapp.com` webhook works under the CSP and nothing else in it survives), and POSTs `{"content": …, "allowed_mentions": {"parse": []}}` as JSON with `credentials: 'omit'`, `referrerPolicy: 'no-referrer'`, `cache: 'no-store'` and `redirect: 'error'`. The content is `chatMessage(moment, {deadline})` with no title (#113), a line break, then the app's origin and `/`: "A new Proposal is open in Reconcile. Voting ends Fri 14 Nov.", "A vote closes within a day in Reconcile." or "A vote has finished in Reconcile.". It is made from the moment and the deadline alone (`botNotice`), so no title, name, count or Document text can reach it.
- **A thread per Proposal** (#123; [ADR-0011](../../../adr/0011-debate-venues.md), ADR-0039: step 1 carries no titles, so threads are numbered, not named). With a forum channel's webhook, each Proposal gets its own Discord thread, and at most three posts in it.
  - **Opening it.** A post for a Proposal with no thread in the log sends `?wait=true` and `thread_name: 'Proposal {number} in Reconcile'` (`botThreadName`). A text channel may refuse that with a 400: the device sends the post once more without it, as above. Or it may post it in the webhook's own channel. Once a post without it is taken, or the message is seen to land in that channel, this device remembers in the group's record (`chatChannel`, by the webhook's id, never its token) that the webhook takes no threads, and posts as above from then on.
  - **Recording it.** Otherwise, when the post was the opening of a Proposal whose winning `open` claim is this device's (`mayRecordThread`), the device reads the webhook's `guild_id` and `channel_id` once per group (`getWebhook`: a GET of `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/{id}/{token}` with no body, cookie, referrer or cache, following no redirect, reading only those two ids), and keeps them in `chatChannel`. When the message's `channel_id` differs from the webhook's, it's a new thread, and the device enqueues `chat-thread {proposal, url: 'https://discord.com/channels/{guild}/{thread}'}`. `state.chatThreads` keeps each kept thread as `{id, seq, received, by, proposal, url}`, and `threadFor(state, proposal)` reads it. A Proposal whose opening went unclaimed, such as the vote that turned the bot on, opens a thread with its first post and records none.
  - **Later posts** for that Proposal add `?thread_id={thread}`. In the thread, the `result` post reads "The argument has concluded. A vote has finished in Reconcile.", then the app's address. A group's posts go out one after another, so each sees what the one before learned.
  - Every request stays on the webhook's own path, so the CSP's `connect-src` needs nothing new: a source's path never matches the query. Deferred past the MVP: thread titles, reading or mirroring a thread, archiving or locking threads, a bot with its own token, and threads on other platforms.
- **What Discord learns** (ADR-0039, §9): that the group uses Reconcile, when its votes open, enter their last day and close, each Proposal's number in the name of its thread (and so how many Proposals the group has made), the app's address (a cross-origin request always carries its origin), and the address of the device that posts, with that browser's ordinary request headers. Never a cookie, a referrer, a title, a name or anything else of the group. The relay sees each claim and each thread link as one more sealed blob, and the thread link points into the group's own Discord server.

Reconcile

verb. To find the ground you share with people you disagree with, and act on it together.

A place for groups to write to the people who follow them, and to join with other groups around what they both hold. What you write travels as far as people vote to carry it, and no further.

Where branches meet

To reconcile is to join around what you share.

Two groups can disagree about most things and still hold one thing in common. When they find it, they reconcile. Each group votes. If both say yes, they form a Coalition, with a mission statement they write together.

Each group keeps its own Documents and its own disagreements. Coalitions can reconcile too, into wider and wider circles.

Dispatches

Write to the people who chose to hear from you.

A Dispatch is a post: an essay, an update, a call to act. It goes to the people who follow your group. From there it travels only by vote.

  1. Write

    A Co-Creator writes a Dispatch and sets how far it may travel.

  2. Your group votes

    If it passes, your group sends it up to its Coalition.

  3. Each Coalition votes

    Level by level, it reaches wider circles of groups that share something with yours.

  4. It stops where agreement stops

    Nothing is broadcast to everyone, and a Dispatch never passes the limit its author set.

A feed that grows from what you've joined and what you share, not from whoever holds your attention longest.

What holds a group up

Living Documents, changed only by vote.

A group keeps its mission statement, its house rules or its points of unity as living Documents. A Co-Creator proposes a change, the group scores it from 0 to 4, and it passes on consent: an average of at least 2.0, with fewer than 10% scoring it 0.

  • 0 Strongly opposed
  • 1 Opposed
  • 2 Neutral
  • 3 Supportive
  • 4 Strongly supportive

The Document isn't the last thing someone typed. It's the last edit that survived a vote.

Below ground

What keeps a forest standing is out of sight.

Your group's Documents, its people and its votes live on your own devices. The relay that carries messages between them holds only sealed envelopes. It can't read them, and it can't list who is in a group.

  • No list of everyone

    There is no directory of people or groups to search or sell.

  • A separate identity for each group

    Being in one group says nothing about the others you're in.

  • Yours to undo

    Joining and following are choices you make, and you can take them back.

The network

A feed that follows your roots.

Trees that look separate pass water and warning through the threads between their roots. Dispatches move the same way: through the groups you've joined and the Coalitions those groups have formed.

  • Reach is voted, never bought

    A Dispatch spreads because groups chose to carry it, one level at a time.

  • Nothing to mine

    No ads, no like counts, no leaderboards. Reconcile runs on gifts.

  • No map of who is joined to whom

    Coalitions form by private invitation from one group to another.

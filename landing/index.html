Reconcile

verb. To find common ground, then act on it together.

Reconcile is a home for all sorts of Groups.

  • Agree on Documents like your mission, values, house rules or action roadmaps democratically.
  • Write posts, called Dispatches, for the people who follow your Groups.
  • Team up with other Groups to form Coalitions when you find common ground and a shared mission.
  • And above all, read and write the Dispatches that spread to higher-level Coalitions when the Groups composing them vote to promote them.

Dispatches

Write to the people who follow you.

A Dispatch is a post. It can be an essay, an update, or a call to act. It goes to everyone who follows your Group. After that, it spreads only by vote.

  1. You write

    Write your Dispatch. Choose how far it is allowed to spread.

  2. Your Group votes

    If your Group says yes, it goes up to your Coalition.

  3. Each Coalition votes

    A Coalition is a team of Groups. Each one votes before passing it on to a bigger one.

  4. It stops when the yeses stop

    Nothing goes out to everyone. And it never goes further than you chose.

Your feed comes from the Groups you joined and what you share with them. Not from whoever is loudest.

Where branches meet

To reconcile is to team up on what you share.

Two Groups can disagree on almost everything and still agree on one thing. When they find it, they can reconcile. Each Group votes. If both say yes, they form a Coalition and write a mission statement together.

Each Group keeps its own Documents and its own opinions. Coalitions can team up too, and form bigger ones.

How a Group decides

Shared Documents change only by vote.

A mission statement is a living Document. So are house rules, or a list of what your Group stands for. Any Co-Creator can propose a change. Then the Group votes. Each person gives the change a score from 0 to 4.

  • 0 Strongly opposed
  • 1 Opposed
  • 2 Neutral
  • 3 Supportive
  • 4 Strongly supportive

A change passes when the average score is 2.0 or higher, and fewer than 1 in 10 voters give it a 0. So a change most people like can still fail, if too many people can't live with it.

The Document is never just the last thing someone typed. It's the last change the Group voted in.

Below ground

Like roots, the important part stays hidden.

Your Group's Documents, members, and votes stay on your own devices. Our server only passes along sealed messages. It can't read them. It can't see who is in your Group.

  • No list of everyone

    There is no list of people or Groups for anyone to search or sell.

  • A different you in each Group

    Joining one Group shows nothing about your other Groups.

  • You can always undo it

    You choose to join or follow. You can leave any time.

The network

A feed that follows your roots.

Trees that look separate are linked underground. Thin threads between their roots carry water and warnings from tree to tree. Dispatches travel the same way: through the Groups you joined and the Coalitions they formed.

  • Votes decide what spreads

    A Dispatch spreads because Groups voted to share it. No one can pay to push it.

  • Nothing built to hook you

    No ads. No like counts. No leaderboards. Reconcile runs on gifts.

  • No public map

    Groups team up by private invitation. No one can look up who has joined with whom.
